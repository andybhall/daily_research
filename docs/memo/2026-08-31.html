Dataset Hunt — 2026-08-31

Frontier: 3. Optimized persuasion text (ads, SMS/robotext, push notifications, email subject lines, A/B-tested fundraising copy) Verdict: AI is now the engine and the medium of political persuasion — the platform threat-report corpus of AI-powered influence operations is a fresh, on-agenda panel nobody has structured.

Revisiting frontier 3 (08-11 took the ad libraries; 08-21 took AI-persuasion experiments). Today's distinct angles: covert AI propaganda caught in the wild, and the brand-new business of advertising inside AI answers.

Top find: AI-powered influence operations (platform threat-report corpus)

Since 2023, OpenAI, Meta, and Google have published recurring threat reports on covert influence operations that used generative AI — the running record of state-actor AI propaganda (OpenAI; Meta Adversarial Threat Reports; Google GTIG).

AI nexus + agenda. Real covert AI-generated political persuasion by state actors — Information bullseye (AI-optimized disinformation), AI nexus intrinsic.

Why it clears the bar. Adversaries deploying AI persuasion under real geopolitical stakes, not a lab (a); threat reports and the operations behind them accreting over time (b); panel-able by operation × actor × platform × tactic × time (d); on-agenda (e5). Unclaimed (c≈3): the general IO datasets (the Twitter/X state-backed archive, AAAI labeled-IO sets) are heavily mined, but the AI-specific threat-report panel is fresh and unstructured.

Evidence — partial probe. Verified this run: OpenAI has disrupted 20+ operations since early 2025 (China/Russia/Iran/North Korea) — its 5 Jun 2025 tranche took down 4 Chinese operations posting across TikTok/Facebook/Reddit/X; Meta finds AI in "virtually every" network it disrupts and on 27 Aug 2026 removed an Iran-linked AI operation (4 Facebook + 31 Instagram accounts). Each report codes actor, country, platforms, tactics, and a Breakout-Scale rating. The catch: these are narrative reports (OpenAI's page 403'd generic fetch), not a structured deposit. Logged in analysis/2026-08-31/. Score: 20/25 (a4 b4 c3 d4 e5).

Access & cost. Reports are public HTML/PDF; a few weeks to code them into a machine-readable operation × actor × tactic panel. Nearest work / difference: analysts summarize individual reports; nobody has built the cross-platform AI-IO panel to ask which actors, tactics, and languages scale — or whether AI actually raises an operation's Breakout-Scale impact. Headline figure: AI-powered influence operations by actor-country and platform, 2023→2026, sized by reach.

Runner-ups

Ads inside AI answers (ChatGPT ads / Google AI Mode). The newest AI-native persuasion medium: OpenAI launched ChatGPT ads on 9 Feb 2026 ("Sponsored" product cards on Free/Go tiers), already ~$100M annualized at $60 CPM across 800M+ weekly users; Google now puts ads on ~25% of AI Overview responses (up from 5.17% in early 2025) plus AI Mode; Perplexity abandoned ads over trust (ChatGPT ads; Perplexity). Persuasion optimized inside the answer itself. Honest caveat: no public ad-transparency archive for chatbot ads yet — a find to build/watch. Score: 18/25 (a4 b3 c4 d3 e5).

Meta "AI Info"-labeled political ads. Meta's Ad Library holds 18M+ US political ad records over seven years and now flags AI-generated creative via an "AI Info" label (self-disclosure + automated detection from 1 Jun 2026; Meta). The AI-labeled subset is a fresh panel of AI adoption in political advertising — though the Ad Library itself is a ledger seed, and API queryability of the AI flag is unconfirmed. Score: 18/25 (a4 b4 c3 d4 e5).
