It's also the same object the 08-27 note already flagged (AI agents as autonomous participants in markets and governance). A build-it-from-Snapshot/Tally project with a labeling gap, not a find.

That's the annual update of the AI shareholder-resolutions object logged on 08-17, and the vote data lives in commercial proxy databases (Glass Lewis, ISS, Boardroom Alpha) — not a new open deposit.
