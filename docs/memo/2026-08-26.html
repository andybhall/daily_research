AI rules in union contracts & labor cases

Where U.S. unions and the NLRB are contesting workplace AI — contract clauses and labor-case filings.

August 26, 2026
20/25
Willis score

The AI-focus gate splits this frontier: the loud signal (AI job postings, AI layoffs) is economic and off-agenda; the on-agenda signal is how AI is governed at work — collective bargaining, algorithmic hiring audits, algorithmic management. None is a clean probe-able deposit today, so figures are verified from sources, not a live pull.

Top find: Union AI provisions & NLRB AI-related filings

The corpus of collectively-bargained AI rules and labor-board disputes over AI — a structured record of how organized labor contests and governs workplace AI (allwork.space; Yale J. on Reg. "Belaboring the Algorithm").

AI nexus + agenda. How workers collectively bargain over and contest AI — Representation (organized worker voice) + Governance (binding contracts governing AI), AI nexus intrinsic.

Why it clears the bar. Real contractual/legal contestation under high stakes, not opinion (a); provisions and charges accreting as AI spreads through workplaces (b); panel-able by union × contract × clause-type × date, and NLRB case × AI (d); on-agenda (e4). Unclaimed (c≈4): law-review commentary abounds but no structured public dataset of union AI clauses + NLRB AI filings exists.

Evidence — could not probe. Verified this run: the NewsGuild holds ~85–90 contracts with explicit AI provisions; the 2023/2026 WGA, SAG-AFTRA and DGA agreements set rules on digital replicas, consent, compensation and semi-annual generative-AI meetings; the ILA won a ban on fully automated technology; and SAG-AFTRA filed an NLRB unfair-labor-practice charge against Llama Productions (May 2025) for replacing workers with AI. NLRB case-search endpoints 404'd here and no deposit exists — the corpus is assemblable from public contracts + NLRB records. Logged in analysis/2026-08-26/. Score: 20/25 (a4 b4 c4 d4 e4).

Access & cost. Union contracts (many public) + NLRB case records; a few weeks to code AI clauses and scrape AI-tagged NLRB filings. Nearest work / difference: essays describe the trend; nobody has built the machine-readable panel of which unions win which AI protections, and how NLRB doctrine on AI-driven layoffs evolves. Headline figure: count of new union AI clauses by industry and protection type, 2023→2026.

Runner-ups

NYC Local Law 144 AEDT bias audits. NYC was first globally to mandate annual race/gender bias audits of automated employment decision tools, publicly posted with selection rates and impact ratios (DCWP). The story is non-compliance: a study of 391 employers found only 18 posted audits (Null Compliance, FAccT 2024), and a Dec-2025 NY Comptroller audit called enforcement "ineffective." The ACLU/NYCLU curate public audits + Perma.cc captures (repo) — curated captures, not a structured CSV. Governance (algorithmic hiring); small + partly claimed. Score: 18/25 (a4 b3 c3 d3 e5).

Worker Info Exchange — algorithmic-management data. WIE processed 500+ subject access requests for gig workers at Amazon Flex, Bolt, Deliveroo, Uber and others, aggregating algorithmic-management and surveillance data into a worker data trust for collective bargaining (workerinfoexchange.org). Behaviorally-revealed AI-management data; Governance (how AI governs workers). Access-limited (worker-held SAR returns). Score: 18/25 (a4 b4 c3 d4 e4).
