This frontier genuinely yields fresh non-US deposits, so I trawled two angles — a fresh non-US election-deepfake corpus and the EU AI Office's new enforcement record — and both come up short of a probe-able open dataset today.

The biggest prospect is the EU AI Act's enforcement machinery, which just switched on. Enforcement powers for the AI Office and national authorities took effect 2 August 2026 (EC enforcement framework), and the Office reports it has begun sending requests for information and has received Article-73 serious-incident reports (EC news, Oct 2026). A serious-incident register or an enforcement-decision register would be a bullseye Governance find (AI incident reporting is named on the agenda). But there is no public register yet — the only tallies are commercial trackers (one counts ~14 actions + 3 inquiries as of Oct 2) with conflicting, unverified figures (e.g., a widely-repeated but unsubstantiated "€47M in August fines"). Too early and not open — a watch, not a find.

The non-US election-deepfake trawl turned up nothing fresh beyond what's logged. For 2026 target countries (Japan, India, Indonesia, Mexico, Philippines, Africa) no open Zenodo/OSF deposit surfaced; the hits were the Brazilian electoral-deepfake corpus already logged (09-24), the tiny synthetic candidates/parties set (rejected 10-07), US-2024 deepfake sets (gated; off-geography), and the unreliable gemmozero/ai-elections-2026 vendor table (HF). The strong logged non-US objects (China CAC, Canada AIA, Brazil jurisprudence) remain the state of the art here.
