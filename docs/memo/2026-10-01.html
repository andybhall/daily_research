Null day — nothing cleared the bar. The screened candidates are recorded below and in the ledger.

Fifth pass on frontier 4 (08-12 HF Hub metadata / Civitai / Pixiv / DSA Transparency DB / Community Notes; 09-01 MCP registries / AI Agent Marketplace / AUP tracking; 09-11 model-card safety disclosures; 09-21 null). The marketplace-and-metadata corners are well-mined, and today's live candidates fail along the now-familiar seam: the clean open archives are off the political agenda, and the on-agenda objects aren't released as open, probe-able datasets. I screened five and promoted none — a null beats a padded find.

The one genuinely open, Willis-genre archive here is off-agenda. AINativeLab/gptstore-data-backup (GitHub) is a real daily archive of OpenAI GPT Store trending data — 565+ commits, snapshots through 2026, name / rank / engagement / rating / category per GPT — but its categories are generic (Writing, Productivity, Programming, DALL·E) with no political, campaign, or government tagging, the same off-agenda wall that killed the Character.AI corpus on 09-21.

The on-agenda objects, meanwhile, don't convert to datasets. TikTok's AIGC labels are the largest synthetic-media exhaust anywhere — TikTok reports having labeled more than 3 billion videos as AI-generated as of July 2026 (report) — but that's an aggregate press figure, not an open per-video dataset to pull. The Midas Project AI Policy Watchtower (watchtower) is squarely on-frontier (ToS/changelog diffs) and on-agenda (model governance and incident reporting) — it logs 60+ dated changes to RSPs, safety frameworks, usage policies, and voluntary commitments across Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI, Meta, and others since December 2022, tagged by change type and severity — but it's a web-only, curated advocacy tracker (~60 entries, no download/API/repo), not open incidental exhaust. And C2PA content credentials — which OpenAI now stamps on every ChatGPT image (Content Credentials) — is an open standard, not a corpus: there's no probe-able collection of manifests to analyze. The fresh political-chatbot evaluation sets (e.g., gemmozero/ai-elections-2026; ISD's 2,400-prompt Chatbots and the Ballot Box) are constructed-to-be-studied research data that collide with the AI election-info audits already logged (08-21).

Pattern note (for the Sunday meta-run, not acted on here): this is the fifth frontier in a row to come up null or near-null on a 4th–5th repeat visit (09-21 F4, 09-28 F1, 09-29 F2, 09-30 F3, today F4). The clean open AI-political datasets on the most-mined frontiers appear largely found; the rotation may be due for a refresh.
