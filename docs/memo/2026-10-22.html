Sixth pass on frontier 5 (08-13 Federal AI Use Case Inventory; 09-02 bills/rulemaking/hearings; 09-22 GAO oversight; 10-02 public comments; 10-12 null). The rich federal AI-governance records are logged. I probed two institutional-record corners earlier passes didn't — government AI usage records and congressional AI witnesses — and both are real but not-yet-deposited.

The standout is genuinely novel: a team from Stanford, USC, KNKX, and the New York Times is FOIA-ing the government's own AI use into the open — 24 federal and 81 state/local requests filed April 2026, already cataloging AI deployments by 53 federal agencies, all 50 states, and 77 local governments, and assembling the first corpus of government AI chatbot logs: 3,216 chat threads, 2023–2026 (actual prompts and responses from government employees' AI tools) (ABA Communications Lawyer). This is dead-on the Governance agenda — how the state actually uses AI, in its own words — and intrinsically AI. But I verified it is not publicly released: these are preliminary findings from an ongoing campaign, and the underlying requests sit on MuckRock as individual, mostly-pending filings (e.g., AI chatbot logs — FCC, plus HHS, USDA, EOUSA). A dispersed, in-flight FOIA campaign, not a probe-able deposit — but exactly the kind of exhaust this project exists to catch, so it's a priority watch.

The second corner, congressional AI hearing witnesses and testimony — whose expertise shapes AI lawmaking — is on-agenda but a build-it-yourself job. The ready-made hearing corpora (C-QUERI, CoCoHD) end at the 117th Congress, before the 2023–2026 AI wave (C-QUERI); current coverage is a small curated list of ~21 AI hearings (The AI Lobby). GovInfo committee XML carries witness names and affiliations, so an AI-hearing-witness × affiliation × date panel is assemblable and would be distinct from the 09-02 hearings count — but no such AI-specific dataset is deposited, and it overlaps that logged find. Shortlist.

Meta-run flag: thirteenth straight null, all on 5th–6th-visit frontiers (last finds 10-02, 10-05). Same structural diagnosis. Note that several recent nulls have surfaced strong watch items pending release (EU Art. 71 DB, CA SB 951, this FOIA corpus) — the pipeline isn't empty, it's waiting on deposits.
