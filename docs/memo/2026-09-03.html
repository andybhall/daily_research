Top find: WikiProject AI Cleanup

A self-organized collaboration of Wikipedia editors detecting, tagging, and cataloguing AI-generated content polluting Wikipedia — unsourced LLM text, fabricated citations, AI-generated images — plus a curated catalog of AI "writing tells" (Wikipedia; 404 Media).

AI nexus + agenda. Volunteers guarding the civic knowledge commons against AI pollution — Information (integrity of the shared information infrastructure democracies rely on), AI nexus intrinsic.

Why it clears the bar. Real AI-generated content caught in the wild by editors under community stakes (a); the flagged-article catalog and tells accrue as the invasion grows (b); panel-able by flagged article × date × AI-tell (d); on-agenda (e5). Unclaimed (c≈4): press coverage abounds (404 Media, Nieman), but the project's catalog has not been mined as a longitudinal dataset of AI's infiltration of Wikipedia.

Evidence — partial probe. Via the Wikipedia API today: the project page is 50,258 bytes of active coordination, and "Category:Articles containing suspected AI-generated texts" currently holds 16 members — but the real catalog is dispersed across dated monthly maintenance categories plus the project's curated lists and tell-catalog, which I did not fully enumerate (honest partial probe). Logged in analysis/2026-09-03/. Score: 20/25 (a4 b4 c4 d3 e5).

Access & cost. Wikipedia is fully open — the project's categories, lists, and full edit histories are queryable via the MediaWiki API; a weekend to assemble the flagged-article panel with revision diffs. Nearest work / difference: journalists profile the editors; nobody has built the time-series of how much AI content reaches Wikipedia, in which topic areas, and how fast volunteers catch it (detection lag). Headline figure: monthly AI-flagged Wikipedia articles by topic and time-to-detection, 2023→2026.

Shortlist (nothing else cleared the political-agenda gate)

  • chub.ai character-card hoard — 2M+ community AI personas in the V2 card format (chub.ai); a massive, genuinely-hobbyist, under-mined hoard, but AI companionship/roleplay, not political (weak agenda).
  • LLM benchmark aggregators (BenchLM, 416 models; BenchmarkAggregator; internal.ai, exportable/daily) — collector-maintained AI-capability panels; derivative of public benchmarks and ledger-adjacent to the OpenRouter/LMArena seeds.
  • Grok controversial-output archives — the "MechaHitler"/political-disinformation incidents (Global Witness) are scattered across news/ADL/Grokipedia, not a single structured hoard; SpeechMap/TrackingAI already cover AI political behavior.
  • NewsGuard AI Content Farm tracker (3,749 AI-slop news sites, 16 languages) — on-agenda but commercial, contact-for-access.
  • "Sources — AI Slop" — a hobbyist companion list to the AI-news-farm trackers; thin.
