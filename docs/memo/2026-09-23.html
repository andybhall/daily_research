The hobbyists stripping safety filters off open AI models

The 1,000+ "uncensored/abliterated" versions of open AI models that individuals publish on Hugging Face — who makes them, and how many millions download guardrail-removed AI.

The un-mined hobbyist object is the supply side: people removing refusal directions from open weights and redistributing them.

Top find: the decensored / "abliterated" open-model ecosystem

On Hugging Face, individuals take open models (Qwen3, Gemma-4, DeepSeek-V4) and "abliterate" them — surgically removing the safety-refusal direction from the weights — then publish the uncensored result. The Heretic tool has made this a factory process (arXiv 2609.05241, "Redistribution as the Persistence Layer"). It is a hobbyist archive in the Derek Willis sense: individuals quietly accumulating a huge, structured, downloadable corpus — here, of AI with its guardrails removed.

AI nexus + agenda. Bullseye Governance — the circumvention and erosion of AI safety controls, the crux of the open-weights policy debate. AI nexus intrinsic; distinct from the jailbreak-prompt hoards in the ledger (this is decensored weights).

Why it clears the bar. Real community behavior — publishing and downloading guardrail-removed models — revealed preference under no compulsion (a4); the corpus accretes continuously on HF (b4); lightly claimed — one arXiv paper documents the ecosystem, but the hobbyist adoption panel (downloads × publisher × base-model × time) is unmined (c3); panel-able exactly that way (d4); on the open-model-safety agenda (e4). Score: 19/25 (a4 b4 c3 d4 e4).

Evidence from a live sample. I queried the HF API this run: searches for "abliterated" and "uncensored" each hit the API cap (>1,000 models each). Deduping the top-500-by-downloads of each = 963 distinct decensored models with ~38 million combined downloads. A handful of hobbyists dominate: HauhauCS 7.2M downloads (24 models), DavidAU 5.6M (38), huihui-ai 4.3M (53), mradermacher 3.9M (324 such models). Single models top 2M downloads each. Artifacts in analysis/2026-09-23/.

Access & cost. Free HF API — model names, downloads, likes, base model, and lastModified per model; a weekend to build the full publisher × base-model × downloads-over-time panel and diff each abliterated model against its safety-tuned parent. Nearest work / difference: the arXiv paper frames the phenomenon; nobody has quantified the adoption curve — which base models get decensored fastest, how downloads track each new frontier open-weight release, and how concentrated the publisher base is. Headline figure: monthly downloads of abliterated models by base-model family, against each open model's release date.
