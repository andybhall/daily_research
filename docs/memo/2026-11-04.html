Null day — nothing cleared the bar. The screened candidates are recorded below and in the ledger.

Sixth pass on frontier 8 (08-26 union AI clauses + NLRB + NYC LL144 + Worker Info Exchange; 09-15 AI-attributed layoffs; 09-25 AI/ML H-1B; 10-05 international CBA AI-clause database; 10-15 null). The on-agenda AI-labor objects are logged, and this frontier's political-agenda fit is the weakest in the rotation. I probed two fresh corners — federal-workforce AI cuts and workplace algorithmic-management disclosure — and neither is a probe-able open dataset.

The federal-workforce AI story is large but unmeasurable as an AI dataset. DOGE-era cuts removed ~9% of the federal workforce (≈238,000 net, per Pew) and agencies are pushing an "AI-first" automation strategy (GovExec; FedSmith), but no dataset separates AI-driven federal job losses from buyouts, deferred resignations, and attrition — Challenger's counts don't break out federal AI cuts, and analysts warn of "AI-washing" of layoffs (The Hill). It's an economic story with a weak political-agenda nexus, overlapping the 09-15 AI-layoffs find.

The workplace algorithmic-management disclosure corner is all law and proposal, not data. Uruguay's Law 20.396/2025 mandates disclosure of automated workplace monitoring and decision systems (tandfonline); California's AB 1898 would require 90 days' notice before deploying a "workplace AI tool" (CA analysis); and an IRPP commentary proposes a Canadian workplace AI registry (Policy Options). These regimes would generate exactly the on-agenda dataset I want — a register of workplace algorithmic-management systems — but none has produced a public registry yet. The academic work (warehouse algorithmic management, surveillance perceptions) is interview/survey research, not open datasets.

Meta-run note: a normal null (last find 10-27). Like several recent frontiers, the live prospects here are regimes pending data release (workplace-AI disclosure registries under Uruguay's law, CA AB 1898, the Canadian proposal) rather than deposits in hand — added to the watch list alongside the EU AI Office enforcement register and the government-AI-chatbot FOIA corpus.
