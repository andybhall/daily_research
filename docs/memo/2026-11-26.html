The one fresh real-world object is a refresh of a logged find. A Purdue team's 2026-midterm AI-political-post database (~482 analyzed fully/partially synthetic posts; Trump in 279 of 482 = 58%) is the 2026 extension of the Political Deepfakes Incident Database (GRAIL Lab / Purdue), already logged (08-18) (CBS Austin) — same curator, same object, grown. Not a new deposit. The other deepfake material is logged or rejected: the USPED 231-item Zenodo set is restricted (reject), the Canadian 2025 arXiv corpus is logged, and Surfshark's cross-country counts are the rejected news-aggregate.

Everything genuinely new is constructed, claimed, or off-object. ShareChat (142,808 in-the-wild chatbot conversations, HF) is the already-rejected general corpus (11-06) — not political-specific. ISD "Chatbots and the Ballot Box" (6 LLMs × 2,400 election prompts) (ISD) and VoteGEO-Bench (generative-engine-optimization electoral visibility) (arXiv 2610.21330) are constructed AI-election-answer audits — the logged genre (08-21 / MIT panel 09-07). Two fresh Zenodo deposits are constructed/replication: a voting-threshold study of committees of 28 AI agents (reasoning traces, not human voters) (Zenodo 21637349) and an Oslo content analysis of AI-election media coverage (NYT + Chosun Ilbo, 2024) (Zenodo 20281727). PolyglotFakeFacts (18-language fake/real news) is a classifier benchmark, not AI-generated-content exhaust (Mendeley). The Tilburg conversational-VAA field study is a constructed experiment behind restricted access (DataverseNL, OpenAccess false) (Tilburg). ICPSR 39673 (survey vote-share) has no AI nexus.

Meta-run note: third frontier-10 null (10-07, 11-16, 11-26), interspersed with the two finds that broke this autumn's streak (Bangladesh 10-27, Voter Prompts 11-06).
