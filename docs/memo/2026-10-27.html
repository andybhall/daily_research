Top find: Bangladesh AI political-misinformation fact-check register (Mendeley)

A Mendeley Data deposit (CC-BY, published 3 Aug 2026) catalogs verified fact-checks of AI-generated/AI-edited political misinformation in Bangladesh's election-cycle information environment, Jan 2025–Feb 2026. It is the non-US, non-English mirror of our US/Brazil/Canada deepfake finds — how a major South Asian democracy's fact-checking ecosystem actually caught and framed election deepfakes.

AI nexus + agenda. Bullseye Information — AI-generated political misinformation and the counter-framing that fact-checkers deploy against it, in a non-US, Bengali-language setting. AI nexus intrinsic; distinct from PDID (08-18, US), the Brazilian jurisprudence corpus (09-24), and the Canadian-election study (08-28).

Why it clears the bar. Real AI-misinfo artifacts verified under fact-checking stakes, not opinion (a4); a curated register accreting across the election cycle, PDID-style rather than incidental (b3); brand-new, single-compiler, Bengali-language, with no secondary analysis — genuinely unclaimed (c4); panel-able by source × synthetic-media format × narrative frame × year × audit status, though small (d3); dead-on agenda and genuinely non-US (e5). Score: 19/25 (a4 b3 c4 d3 e5).

Evidence from the record (fetched today). The register holds 122 item-level fact-check records, each a unique verified claim involving AI-generated or AI-edited visuals/audio tied to a political actor or election process, plus 13 aggregate monitoring sources for context and 11 records flagged for review. The cases come from five Bangladeshi fact-checkers — Rumor Scanner (110), Dismislab (6), FactWatch (4), BOOM (1), Ajker Patrika (1) — a striking concentration showing how thin the verified-AI-misinfo pipeline is outside one dominant outlet. Each record is coded by synthetic-media format, dominant narrative frame, and audit status. Artifacts in analysis/2026-10-27/.

Access & cost. One CC-BY download (item-level register + aggregate sources); each record links to the originating fact-check. A weekend to build the format × frame × outcome panel and test which AI-misinfo frames recur across the cycle, and how the fact-checking response concentrated. Nearest work / difference: the compiler catalogs the fact-checks; nobody has analyzed the narrative-frame distribution or the single-outlet concentration of Bangladesh's AI-misinfo verification, or compared it to the US/Brazil/Canada records we hold.
