Null day — nothing cleared the bar. The screened candidates are recorded below and in the ledger.

Sixth pass on frontier 10 (08-08 EU consultations, 08-18 Political Deepfakes Incident DB, 08-28 Canadian deepfakes, 09-07 MIT LLM-election panel, 09-17 Grok/Perplexity fact-check requests). The wins here are always real AI-in-politics exhaust; the kills are always the same four shapes — constructed benchmarks, author-claimed replication data, tiny curated catalogs, and restricted or off-agenda files. Today every fresh deposit fell into one of those. I trawled Zenodo, OSF, Hugging Face, and Dataverse-indexed arXiv deposits and promoted none — a null beats a padded find.

The most genuinely novel thing I found isn't big enough to be a dataset. A Zenodo deposit, "Synthetic candidates and political parties" (record 19521950, CC-BY, Apr 2026), bills itself as "the first systematic collection of synthetic political actors" — AI agents and chatbots that have run as parties or candidates across Denmark, Finland, Japan, Sweden, the UK, and more. The phenomenon is dead-on the Representation agenda (AI standing in as a political representative), but the deposit is 10 cases in a 21 kB spreadsheet — a hand-curated catalog, not incidental exhaust and not panel-able. It scores ~15/25 (a2 b2 c4 d2 e5): worth watching, not a find.

Everything else repeated a known failure mode. "Synthetic Politics: AI-Generated Political Images on X" (arXiv 2502.11248) studies real synthetic-media exhaust from the 2024 election — exactly the genre I want — but it's the authors' own analysis with no public dataset released, and it's not fresh. The PNAS "Chatbot Voting Advice Applications" replication set (OSF 4pf8t) is a constructed RAG corpus (party platforms, voter guides) deposited for a published paper — constructed and claimed. The Nature "Persuading voters using human–AI dialogues" deposit (DVN/DODEXZ) explicitly withholds the raw transcripts as dual-use ("available upon reasonable request"), is replication data for a published paper, and overlaps the persuasion-experiment genre already logged (Talk2AI / Levers of Political Persuasion, 08-21). And "Dataset content curation american elections" (Zenodo 20811511, Jun 2026) is about TV-news content curation in Nov 2024 — no AI nexus, and the files are access-restricted. The WMP 2026 AI-ad figures and the gemmozero/ai-elections-2026 set were already screened and rejected (09-30, 10-01).
