Fourth pass on frontier 3 (08-11 Snap/FCC/TikTok ad libraries; 08-21 Talk2AI, Levers of Political Persuasion, AI election-info audits; 08-31 AI influence-ops, ads-in-AI-answers, Meta AI-labeled ads). The frontier's AI-persuasion corners are now well-mined, and today's fresh candidates all fail on the same two axes: they are synthetically constructed to be studied (not incidental exhaust), or they collide with datasets already in the ledger. I screened 15 and promoted none — a null day beats a padded one.

The pattern was consistent. The new AI-persuasion corpora are detector benchmarks — synthetic messages generated to train classifiers (the same reason OpenFake and the audio-deepfake detection sets were killed). The new AI-persuasion studies are lab experiments (elicited, one-shot), and we already hold the two strongest of that genre — the Levers of Political Persuasion (Science 2025) and Talk2AI. The real deployed-persuasion exhaust on this frontier — Meta's AI-labeled political ads, the platform influence-operation threat reports, ads inside AI answers — is already Found. No new door opened today.
