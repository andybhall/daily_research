Optimized persuasion text (ads, SMS/robotext, push notifications, email subject lines, A/B-tested fundraising copy)

Snap's Political Ads Library — nine years of political ad creative with spend, impressions, and 20+ targeting fields per ad, the under-mined third to the Meta/Google seeds.

21/25
Willis score

A heavily-mined frontier: the best persuasion-text corpora are already seeds (Meta/Google ad libraries, Princeton political emails) or claimed benchmarks (Upworthy, Change.org, GoFundMe). What survived is the ad-transparency exhaust nobody has worked at the creative-panel level.

Top find: Snap Political & Advocacy Ads Library

Every political/advocacy ad served on Snapchat since 2018, published as yearly bulk files (snap.com/political-ads). Each ad row carries the creative URL (the copy), spend, impressions, start/end dates, the paying advertiser/committee, and ~20 targeting columns (geo, interests, segments, age/gender, advanced demographics) — persuasion text plus the optimization outcomes and the microtargeting behind it.

Why it clears the bar. Ad creative bought under electoral stakes is revealed behavior, not stated preference (a); the library accumulates incidentally as a transparency mandate, nine years deep (b); advertisers recur across cycles (d); it maps cleanly to Information — political persuasion and advertising (e).

Unclaimed — honestly. Not virgin: a handful of papers use it (Tanusondjaja et al. 2023, ~11,837 ads; a 2024 Meta/Google/Snap comparison). But it is far less mined than the Meta/Google seeds, and the creative-text × microtargeting × spend panel is essentially unworked — criterion c is the discount (≈3/5).

Evidence from a live sample. I pulled the 2024 and 2026 bulk files today and analyzed them: 23,027 ads in 2024 from 626 paying advertisers, $29.4M in USD spend, 3.77 billion impressions, and 86% of ads using geo/interest/segment microtargeting; 2026-YTD adds 5,121 ads. Crucially, 68 advertisers appear in both the 2024 and 2026 files — a real cross-cycle panel, not a snapshot. Top 2024 spender by ad count: Harris for President (6,470 ads). Script + tables in analysis/2026-08-11/.

Access & cost. Direct ZIP per year, no auth (storage.googleapis.com/ad-manager-political-ads-dump/political/&lt;YEAR&gt;/PoliticalAds.zip); metadata is a weekend, but fetching + OCR-ing the linked creatives to study the copy is a few weeks. Nearest work / our difference: prior papers describe spend/targeting; nobody has linked creative wording to microtargeting segment to build a "which message to whom" map across cycles. Headline figure: message-frame × targeted-segment heatmap, 2018→2026. Score: 21/25 (a5 b4 c3 d4 e5).

Runner-ups

FCC OPIF Political Files. Every US broadcast/cable station uploads its political ad-buy records to the FCC's Online Public Inspection File, queryable via the FCC OPIF search API. Behaviorally revealed ad spend under electoral stakes, longitudinal and panel-able by station/candidate/committee, and genuinely under-mined since ProPublica's crowdsourced Free the Files (2012) precisely because the filings are scanned PDFs. Straddles frontier 2 (money) but is squarely "ads." Not probed today (PDF-heavy). Score: 21/25 (a4 b5 c4 d4 e4).

TikTok Commercial Content Library. DSA-mandated repository of paid ads with creative, run dates, targeting parameters, and impressions, via a Commercial Content API. Fresh and under-mined, panel-able by advertiser — but TikTok bans political ads (so this is commercial persuasion), the API is gated to approved researchers and EU-first, with no spend field. Weakest agenda fit of the three. Score: 19/25 (a4 b4 c4 d4 e3).

Shortlist

  • SnorCall robocall corpus — 232,723 robocalls transcribed from a 66,606-line honeypot (USENIX Security 2023); ideal exhaust, but the authors state the corpus cannot be released → dead on access.
  • News push-notification corpora — only small hand-collected study samples (639–2,085 alerts); no large open longitudinal dataset exists.
  • Upworthy Research Archive — 32,487 headline A/B tests (Nature Sci Data); a famous, heavily-used benchmark → Rejected.
  • Change.org petitions — text + signature outcomes, but petition-success prediction is a saturated subfield → Rejected.
  • GoFundMe crowdfunding copy — text + funding outcomes, extensively studied (moral-framing, cancer-campaign papers) → Rejected.
