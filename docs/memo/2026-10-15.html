Null day — nothing cleared the bar. The screened candidates are recorded below and in the ledger.

Fifth pass on frontier 8 (08-26 US union AI clauses + NLRB + NYC LL144 audits + Worker Info Exchange; 09-15 AI-attributed layoffs; 09-25 AI/ML H-1B; 10-05 international CBA AI-clause database). Every on-agenda leg is logged — what unions bargain, how the labor board fights, how hiring is audited, who gets imported, who gets cut. Today's fresh corners fail the same two ways this frontier always fails: the new disclosure regimes are returning nothing, and the live story isn't a dataset (and barely touches the political agenda).

The WARN-notice angle is a clean null result, not a find. New York added a field asking whether "technological innovation or automation" drove a layoff — and in the first year, across more than 160 companies filing WARN notices, not one attributed its layoffs to AI or automation (Hunton). California's SB 951 (signed Sept 30, 2026) adds the same AI-driven-layoff disclosure to Cal/WARN, but it isn't effective until Jan 1, 2027, so there are no filings yet (Nat. Law Review). This is the same pattern as California's AB 302 ADS inventory (10-12): a self-reported AI-disclosure mandate that so far yields an empty column. The open WARN datasets that exist (layoffdata.com; a daily Hugging Face WARN set) are general layoff data with no reliable AI tag, and the AI-layoff trackers that claim one are commercial, self-reported, and overlap the 09-15 find.

The genuinely live AI-labor story isn't on the agenda and isn't deposited. The AI supply chain's data workers are organizing — Kenya's Data Labelers Association drew 339 members in its first week, Sama's Nairobi ChatGPT annotators alleged psychological harm at under $2/hour, and Surge AI faces a misclassification class action (OpenAI and unions, Wikipedia; Surge AI, Wikipedia) — but this lives in journalism, Wikipedia, and litigation, not an open dataset, and it's a labor-conditions story more than a political-information/representation/governance one. No new door opened.

Meta-run flag: eighth straight null (10-06 through 10-15, all 5th–6th-visit frontiers; last finds 10-02, 10-05). Same diagnosis as yesterday — the ~10-day revisit cadence is re-surfacing not-yet-live, zero-entry, or commercial candidates. Flagging for the Sunday meta-run, not acting unilaterally.
