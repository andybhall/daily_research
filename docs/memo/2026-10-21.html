Sixth pass on frontier 4 (08-12 HF Hub / Civitai / Pixiv / DSA DB / Community Notes; 09-01 MCP registries / AI Agent Marketplace / AUP tracking; 09-11 model-card safety; 09-21 null; 10-01 null). The marketplace/metadata corners are logged and the frontier was null on both prior visits, so I dug two sub-territories earlier passes never touched — and both came closer than anything in days, without clearing.

The strongest is the news-vs-AI crawler fight: at scale, political-information producers are walling off AI crawlers in robots.txt. A July 2026 snapshot of ~4,223 parsed files names GPTBot (633 domains), CCBot (567), ClaudeBot (563), and Google-Extended (522) as the most-blocked agents (TechnologyChecker); a Canadian study found 70% of news sites block AI crawlers (GlobeNewswire). It's intrinsically AI, incidental (robots.txt are deployed signals captured longitudinally by the Internet Archive / Common Crawl), and strongly panel-able (domain × crawler × date). But the one clean deposited open version is the Data Provenance Initiative's "Consent in Crisis" — a famous mid-2024 audit of 14,000 domains, heavily cited and team-published (DPI) — which fails "unclaimed" badly, is 2+ years old, and is framed as training-data consent (IP/data policy), not political information. The fresher 2026 reads are vendor/proprietary (Cloudflare) or self-reported blogs; the genuinely open fresh version is a build-from-raw Common-Crawl/IA project, not a deposit. Strong revisit candidate, not today's find.

The second, AI-search source-citation for political queries, is on-agenda (which outlets AI search elevates when people ask about elections) but is constructed audits that overlap the AI-election-info-audits already logged (08-21). AlgorithmWatch ran 4,480 queries on 2026 German state elections and found ~half of links from ten domains, YouTube most-cited (the-decoder); States United audited US state-election answers; VoteGEO-Bench measures electoral visibility. These are query-set studies, mostly not deposited, and a refinement of a logged genre — not a new open exhaust object.

Meta-run flag: twelfth straight null, all on 5th–6th-visit frontiers (last finds 10-02, 10-05). Same structural diagnosis, thoroughly documented across prior memos; still flagging rather than editing the rotation unilaterally.
