Fourth consecutive null here. Four days before the midterms the AI-persuasion story is saturating coverage, but — as on every prior visit — it doesn't convert to an open, AI-labeled corpus.

The one fresh, open artifact is off-frontier. Pew Research (Oct 29, 2026) analyzed 20,751 Facebook/Instagram ads about data centers using the Meta Ad Library API and a classifier, with a CSV download (Pew). It's a genuinely interesting political-information signal — the AI build-out's data centers are now a backlash theme in political advertising — but these are human-made ads that mention AI infrastructure, not AI-generated/optimized persuasion (this frontier's object), it's Pew's topic-level analysis rather than a released creatives corpus, and it's closer to the data-center-subsidies find (09-09) than to persuasion text.

Everything else repeated known kills. The Wesleyan Media Project's AI-ad tracking is now ~$80M across ~170 AI ads (MinnPost) but publishes only aggregates — no ad-level release (rejected 09-30, 10-20); its 2022 CREATIVE cross-platform dataset has no confirmed 2026 equivalent yet. gemmozero/ai-elections-2026 remains a thin, undocumented vendor table (HF). AgoraSpeech is Greek election speeches with ChatGPT annotations (not AI-generated persuasion), and the Nature human–AI persuasion-dialogue replication data withholds the raw transcripts (rejected 10-07). The raw Meta (390k+ ads since Oct 1) and Google ad libraries remain open, but turning them into an AI-generated-persuasion dataset is still a build-it-and-label-it project, not a deposit.

Meta-run note: fourth straight frontier-3 null; the AI-persuasion-text corners are comprehensively logged or structurally un-releasable (aggregates, withheld dual-use data, build-your-own ad-library labeling). Same diagnosis; flagging, not editing the rotation.
