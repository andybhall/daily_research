Seventh pass on frontier 2 (08-10 FARA; 08-20 USASpending AI procurement + LDA lobbying + Open Phil philanthropy; 09-09 Leading the Future super PAC + Good Jobs First subsidies; 09-29 null; 10-09 null; 10-19 null). The clean open AI-money datasets are comprehensively logged across every sub-type the frontier names, and this is the fourth consecutive null here. Five days before the midterms I checked the one sub-territory left — Form 990s of AI-advocacy nonprofits — and the rest of the landscape, and found nothing new to pull.

The 990 corner doesn't convert. ProPublica's Nonprofit Explorer is an open, digitized Form 990 database, but 501(c)(3) 990 filings run a year or more behind, the electioneering money flows through 501(c)(4)s that report differently, and the AI-advocacy slice is the Open Philanthropy philanthropy object already logged (08-20). Treasury's April-2026 Form 990 revision (EisnerAmper) is a disclosure-policy change, not a dataset. No clean panel of AI-political nonprofit spending exists.

Everything else is logged or commercial. gemmozero/ai-lobbying-money-2026 (HF) is a sub-1K-row JSON set, mostly 2024 amounts, no methodology — the same vendor whose election and litigation sets I rejected on 10-01 and 10-08. The AI Lobby (TheDataProject.AI) and OpenLobby (OpenLobby) are advocacy/commercial repackagings of Senate LDA filings — the object logged 08-20 and rejected again 10-19 — with unsourced headline figures ($225M vs. a projected $900M+ for 2026). No distinct new AI-money object outside the logged procurement / lobbying / philanthropy / campaign-finance / subsidy set surfaced.

Meta-run note: fourth straight frontier-2 null; this frontier's AI corners are the most thoroughly exhausted in the rotation (I even dug the federal-AI-grants sub-territory on 10-19). The live energy remains in watch items pending release (the Stanford/USC government-AI-chatbot-logs FOIA corpus), not new money-trail deposits. Same structural diagnosis; flagging rather than editing the rotation.
