October 6, 2026
Null day — nothing cleared the bar. The screened candidates are recorded below and in the ledger.

Fourth pass on frontier 9 (08-17 AI shareholder resolutions + proxy votes, Manifold, Metaculus; 08-27 10-K AI-risk disclosures + AI market-agents + Bittensor; 09-16 board AI-oversight proxy disclosures). The corporate-governance leg is now thoroughly mined — and its regulatory substrate is being removed. Today's candidates fail the usual seam: the on-agenda objects are already logged or no longer accruing, and the genuinely fresh object has no clean, labeled, open dataset. I screened the frontier and promoted none — a null beats a padded find.

Two corporate-governance angles collapsed on contact. SEC no-action letters on AI shareholder proposals (the resolution-disputes corner) looked promising, but the SEC's Division of Corporation Finance permanently stopped responding to Rule 14a-8 no-action requests (announced Aug 14, 2026, making permanent a Nov 2025 pause) and on Aug 28, 2026 sent OMB a proposed rule to rescind Rule 14a-8 entirely (MoFo; Skadden) — so no AI-exclusion-dispute dataset is accruing behind it. And anti-ESG / AI shareholder proposals are a real 2026 story — AI landed on Alphabet, Meta, IBM, and Walmart ballots (IBM's failed at 2.4%), while anti-ESG proposals were the most-filed E&S type yet averaged ~1.7% support and none passed (Harvard corpgov; Boardroom Alpha) — but that's the shareholder-resolutions object already logged (08-17), and the vote data lives in commercial proxy databases (ISS, Boardroom Alpha).

The genuinely fresh object is striking but doesn't convert. AI agents now trade political prediction markets at scale — CoinDesk reports more than 30% of Polymarket wallets using AI agents, with ~37% of agents profitable versus 7–13% of humans, and agents like Olas's Polystrat running 4,200+ trades in a month (CoinDesk). The market data itself is wide open — Polymarket is on-chain, with official APIs, tick-level history, and Dune dashboards (Awesome Prediction Market Tools). But the clean, open layer (prices, trades, wallets) isn't intrinsically about AI, and the intrinsically-AI layer — which wallets are AI agents, and their P&L — isn't a clean labeled dataset: the 30% figure is a vendor estimate (LayerHub; "data shared by the team"), and identifying agent wallets on-chain is contested heuristics, not a deposited label. It's also adjacent to the 08-27 AI-market-agents/Bittensor note, and its tie to the political agenda (AI bots shaping the market-implied election odds that media report) is real but a step removed. A research project with a labeling problem, not a probe-able find.
