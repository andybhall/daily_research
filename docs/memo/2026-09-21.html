Null day — nothing cleared the bar. The screened candidates are recorded below and in the ledger.

Fourth pass on frontier 4 (08-12 HF Hub metadata / Civitai / Pixiv; 09-01 MCP registries / AI Agent Marketplace / AUP tracking; 09-11 model-card safety disclosures). The marketplace-and-metadata corners are well-mined, and today's fresh candidates split cleanly into two failure modes: great exhaust that's off the political agenda, or on-agenda objects that aren't released as open datasets. I screened ~15 and promoted none — a null beats a padded find.

The tension was sharp with the day's most impressive object. A new large-scale scrape of Character.AI (3–5.7M user-built bots with creator, chats, likes, and the character-defining text) is textbook platform exhaust — but the ecosystem is anime, games, and fictional-romance roleplay; the authors report no prominent political or ideological personas, so it illuminates parasocial companionship, not political information, representation, or governance. It's also not openly released (academic-gated, on acceptance). The genuinely on-agenda option — mandatory AI-content disclosure labels on TikTok/YouTube — has no open dataset behind the platform stats (TikTok says 1.3B+ videos auto-labeled; studies are tiny hand-collected samples), so there's nothing to pull. And the AI model release/pricing trackers are market-structure exhaust with a weak political nexus. No door opened today.
