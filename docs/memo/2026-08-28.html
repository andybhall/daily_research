Same vise as 08-18: on the dataverse trawl, the AI deposits that are accessible are constructed (benchmarks, audits, annotated corpora), while the genuinely incidental political-AI data isn't cleanly deposited. I surface the one real result and log the near-misses honestly rather than pad.

Top find: Deepfakes in the 2025 Canadian Election (measurement)

A ACM Web Conference 2026 study (Livernoche, Arodi, Musulan, Yang, Salvail, Marceau Caron, Godbout, Rabbany) that measured AI-generated imagery across X, Bluesky, and Reddit during the 2025 Canadian federal election (arXiv 2512.13915; ACM).

AI nexus + agenda. Real AI-generated political imagery circulating in a real election — Information (AI-distorted political information), AI nexus intrinsic; non-US.

Why it (barely) clears the bar. Deepfakes deployed in the wild under electoral stakes, not a lab set (a); measured across platforms in real time (b); panel-able by platform × partisanship × time (d); on-agenda (e5). Unclaimed (c≈3): fresh (2026) and non-US, though deepfake-prevalence measurement is an active area and it is genre-adjacent to the US-focused PDID logged 08-18.

Evidence — verified, partial access. The study analyzed 187,778 posts; 5.9% of election-related images were deepfakes, and right-leaning accounts shared them far more often (9.2% of their images flagged vs 3.9% for left-leaning), with flagged content skewing defamatory/conspiratorial. The honest catch: the released artifact is the constructed OpenFake detector-training set (3M real + 1M synthetic images), not the in-the-wild election corpus, whose public deposit I could not confirm from the paper. Logged in analysis/2026-08-28/. Score: 20/25 (a4 b4 c3 d4 e5).

Access & cost. Detector + OpenFake are open; the in-the-wild election corpus likely needs an author request. Nearest work / difference: PDID curates US political-deepfake incidents; this measures prevalence and partisan asymmetry in one non-US election — the unbuilt object is a multi-country, multi-election in-the-wild deepfake panel with partisanship. Headline figure: deepfake share of election imagery by platform and account lean, across successive national elections.

  • LLM voting-advice / political-bias audits (EU VAA, 7,400 responses across DE/NL/LU/CZ; parliamentary-record bias, arXiv 2601.08785) → Rejected: constructed audits, crowded area, adjacent to the 08-21 AI-election-audits and 08-24 TrackingAI finds.
  • Pangram cross-platform AIGC corpus (1,002,627 posts, LinkedIn/Medium/Substack/X/Reddit) — commercial opt-in scans, access unclear, general (not political) AI content.
  • AgoraSpeech (Zenodo) — political discourse annotated by humans + AI; a constructed annotated corpus.
  • "AI helps find common ground in deliberation" (Science, Habermas Machine) — landmark but constructed + heavily claimed.
  • ShareChat (arXiv 2512.17843) — real shared chatbot conversations; the political subset overlaps the 08-18 human-AI-political-chats find.
