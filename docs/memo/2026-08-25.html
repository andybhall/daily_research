The AI-focus gate finds a distinctive non-US object: how other states govern AI. China's mandatory registry is the standout; Europe's public-sector AI registers and India's AI-in-elections trail behind on cleanliness.

Top find: China CAC generative-AI / algorithm registry

Every public-facing generative-AI service in China — text, image, audio, video, multimodal — must register with the Cyberspace Administration (CAC) before deployment; the filings are published as the world's only comprehensive, publicly accessible generative-AI registry (Gradient Flow; Trivium).

AI nexus + agenda. A live map of how a major state licenses and controls AI — bullseye Governance (AI regulation/registration), non-US/non-English, AI nexus intrinsic.

Why it clears the bar. Companies filing under hard regulatory stakes — register or you cannot legally deploy (a); regulatory exhaust accreting monthly (b); panel-able by service × company × province × time (d); on-agenda (e5). Unclaimed (c≈3): China-watchers (Trivium, Basilinna, Gradient Flow) work it and a China/UK comparison exists (arXiv 2606.00035), but rigorous academic panel analysis of China's AI-industry structure is thin.

Evidence — partial probe. Each filing carries registration number, approval date, tool name, company, province, functional description, and business model (B2B/B2C). Scale verified this run: 3,739 tools from ~2,353 companies (Apr 2025) → 5,822 entries from 3,787 entities (Nov 2025), +250–300/month, including DeepSeek and Ernie. I could not pull the raw list: the portal API (beian.cac.gov.cn/api/algorithmpublicity/list) returns 200 but errno:440 "please log in first", and www.cac.gov.cn was unreachable here — analysts work from CAC's periodic bulk Excel releases. Logged in analysis/2026-08-25/. Score: 23/25 (a5 b5 c3 d5 e5).

Access & cost. Official Excel announcements (Chinese) + scrapes; a couple of weeks to build the translated panel. Nearest work / difference: analysts count filings and hunt "the next DeepSeek"; nobody has built the province × time × model-lineage panel of China's state-gated AI industry. Headline figure: monthly new generative-AI filings by province and modality, 2023→2026.

Runner-ups

European public-sector AI registers (beyond the NL register). Germany's "marketplace of AI opportunities" held 1,305 registered AI projects at extraction; France runs four public-algorithm repositories (incl. France Travail); the EU JRC maintains a Public Sector Tech database (OECD state-of-the-art report). A non-US algorithmic-government panel, complementary to the NL register (logged 08-13) — but fragmented across countries and maturities. Score: 19/25 (a4 b4 c3 d4 e5).

India AI-in-elections. In the 2024 general election, parties spent ~US$50M on authorized AI-generated campaign content and ~40 campaigns used generative AI; Google's Shakti fact-checking collective (300+ journalists, 50+ newsrooms) found only ~2% of reviewed stories were deepfakes/AI (TechPolicy.Press). Non-US AI-in-elections, but the evidence is scattered across fact-checks and reports, not a single deposit. Score: 18/25 (a4 b3 c3 d3 e5).
