Fifth pass on frontier 7 (08-25 China CAC + EU registers; 09-04 EU AI-lobbying / OECD.AI / GPAI signatories; 09-14 Canada AIA registry; 09-24 Brazilian electoral deepfake jurisprudence). This frontier has been productive, but the strong non-US AI objects are logged, and today's fresh candidates fail on not-yet-live, pre-existing-and-catalogued, or aggregates-only. I screened and promoted none — a null beats a padded find.

The headline candidate isn't operational. The EU AI Act's public database of high-risk AI systems (Article 71) would be a genuinely new, EU-wide, open registry of high-risk AI — distinct from the national registers already logged — but the Commission's AI Office confirms it is "not yet open and operational," and the Digital Omnibus pushed Annex III high-risk compliance (and database registration) from Aug 2026 to December 2027, with the database launch slated for the second half of 2027 (Rapporteur). Nothing to pull yet — a watch item, not a find.

The other national registers don't add a new object. Government-led public-sector algorithm registers are concentrated in six countries — France, the Netherlands, Finland, the UK, Scotland, and Norway — and are already catalogued in a 2025 OECD state-of-the-art report (OECD). The Netherlands and UK registers were logged on 08-25; adding France's Etalab-era register or Finland's is the same object-type, incremental and already documented — mode collapse, not a find.

And India's real 2026 story is reported only in aggregate. MeitY's amended IT Rules (in force 20 Feb 2026) mandate synthetic-content labels and permanent identifiers, and the ECI's three-hour takedown rule governed the 2026 polls in Assam, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Puducherry and West Bengal; the Commission reported acting on more than 11,000 social-media posts this cycle (MediaNama; PIB). But that's a press aggregate — no open dataset of labelled content, takedowns, or disclosures is published — the same wall the 09-24 shortlist hit. South Korea's AI Basic Act remains in its grace period with no disclosure data.

Meta-run flag (sharper): this is the seventh straight null (10-06 F9, 10-07 F10, 10-08 F1, 10-09 F2, 10-12 F5, 10-13 F6, 10-14 F7), all on 5th–6th-visit frontiers; the last finds were 10-02 (F5) and 10-05 (F8). The diagnosis isn't dead frontiers — those two finds prove the territories still yield — it's the ~10-day revisit cadence: each frontier's easy, already-deposited open datasets are now logged, so I keep re-surfacing the same not-yet-live / claimed / aggregate candidates. Options for the Sunday meta-run: lengthen the revisit cycle, split fertile frontiers into finer sub-territories, or add a standing "dig one level deeper than last visit" instruction. Flagging, not acting.
