# Dataset Hunt — 2026-09-30

**Frontier:** 3. Optimized persuasion text (ads, SMS/robotext, push notifications, email subject lines, A/B-tested fundraising copy)
**Verdict:** Null day — nothing cleared the bar.

Fifth pass on frontier 3 (08-11 Snap/FCC/TikTok ad libraries; 08-21 Talk2AI, Levers of Political Persuasion, AI election-info audits; 08-31 AI influence-ops, ads-in-AI-answers, Meta AI-labeled ads; 09-10 null). The AI-persuasion corners here are well-mined, and today's live candidates split cleanly into two failing halves: **the objects with an intrinsic AI nexus have no open dataset, and the objects with a clean open dataset have no intrinsic AI nexus.** I screened the frontier and promoted none — a null beats a padded find.

The 2026 midterms are the first real "AI ads" cycle, so the story is loud, but it doesn't convert to a probe-able dataset. The **Wesleyan Media Project** is the measurement of record — its 2026-09-04 release ([WMP](https://mediaproject.wesleyan.edu/releases-090426/)) reports a minimum of **164 AI-generated or AI-enhanced political ads, just under $80M in spend, 80% from Republican sponsors, and 69% carrying no AI disclosure** — but the release publishes **aggregates only**: no ad-level table, spend via licensed AdImpact, and access to underlying data by emailing the project. It's also the opposite of an unclaimed hoard — a flagship academic shop whose own team publishes it. The compliance angle is sharp (per the [AP](https://www.clickondetroit.com/news/politics/2026/09/25/ai-ads-are-popular-in-the-2026-midterms-but-state-laws-havent-stopped-a-lack-of-disclosure/), only **29% of AI ads in states that require disclosure actually disclosed**), but a sharp finding isn't an open dataset.

The clean open corpora on this frontier, meanwhile, aren't about AI. The **Archive of Political Emails** ([politicalemails.org](https://politicalemails.org/messages); [dwillis/political-emails](https://github.com/dwillis/political-emails)) is a genuine Willis-genre hoard — 2M+ political fundraising emails since 2019, updated daily as JSONL — but it's persuasion text in general, with only an indirect AI angle (you *could* hunt AI-written copy in it), the same rule-6 failure that killed generic corpora on 09-10, and its genre is already seeded (Princeton's 2020 email corpus). And **AI-personalized political SMS** — bots trained to text like candidates, holding thousands of simultaneous "conversations" ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/07/12/nx-s1-5867763/ai-artificial-intelligence-data-texts-bots-voters-campaigns)) — has an intrinsic AI nexus and is dead-on agenda, but it's vendor campaign practice with **no released corpus** to pull.
