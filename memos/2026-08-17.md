# Dataset Hunt — 2026-08-17

**Frontier:** 9. Markets & governance (prediction markets, DAO votes, proxy voting, shareholder resolutions, resolution disputes)
**Verdict:** AI shareholder resolutions — investors are now voting on how Big Tech governs its AI, a company × year proxy panel that's fresh, behaviorally revealed, and unbuilt.

## Top find: AI shareholder resolutions & proxy voting

Since 2022 a distinct class of shareholder proposals demands AI governance from public companies — reports on AI-driven misinformation, board oversight of AI, human-rights/ethics due diligence. In the 2024–25 proxy years there were **15 AI resolutions, 12 of them at Alphabet, Amazon, Apple, Meta, and Microsoft**, averaging ~30% support (nearly double the 16% for E&S proposals), with AI-misinformation proposals hitting **53.6% at Meta and 45.7% at Alphabet** among independent shareholders ([Sustainalytics/corpgov](https://corpgov.law.harvard.edu/2025/05/30/investor-views-on-ai-oversight-what-do-proxy-votes-tell-us/); [Open MIC](https://www.openmic.org/generative-artificial-intelligence-misinformation-and-disinformation)).

**AI nexus + agenda.** Investors contesting how firms build and deploy AI — squarely **Governance** (the agenda explicitly lists corporate governance), AI nexus intrinsic.

**Why it clears the bar.** Proposals filed and votes cast under real financial stakes, not stated preference (a); proxy-season disclosure exhaust accruing annually (b); panel-able by company × year × proposal-type × outcome, with fund-level votes in Form N-PX (d). **Unclaimed (c≈4):** trends are tracked by advocacy/law firms ([FTI](https://fticommunications.com/unveiling-key-trends-in-ai-shareholder-proposals/)) and the general ISS Voting Analytics DB exists, but no academic *panel of AI-specific proposals with outcomes* has been built.

**Evidence from a live sample.** Via the SEC EDGAR full-text-search API today I counted proxy statements (DEF 14A) mentioning "artificial intelligence": **154 (2019) → 431 (2022) → 794 (2024) → 1,152 (2025) → 1,432 (2026)** — a ~9× rise; AI has swept into the proxy. The exact proposal language is far rarer (`"report on artificial intelligence"`: 0–2/yr), confirming the proposal panel must be assembled from EDGAR + N-PX + trackers, not a single feed. Yearly counts in [`analysis/2026-08-17/`](../analysis/2026-08-17/SUMMARY.md). **Score: 22/25** (a5 b4 c4 d4 e5).

**Access & cost.** EDGAR full-text search + bulk N-PX are free APIs; assembling the labeled AI-proposal panel with vote outcomes is a few weeks. **Nearest work / difference:** commentary describes the *trend*; nobody has built the machine-readable panel linking proposal text → filer → company → vote → subsequent AI-governance disclosure. **Headline figure:** support rate for AI-governance proposals vs. all E&S proposals, by year and target company.

## Runner-ups

**Manifold Markets AI questions.** Play-money prediction market with a fully open API and bulk dumps since Dec 2021 ([API](https://docs.manifold.markets/api)). **Probed today:** 838 AI markets, 43% resolved, **30.5M mana volume**, 379 creators, creation peaking in 2023 (346) — wall-to-wall AGI-timeline and AI-regulation questions. Behaviorally-revealed AI expectations, panel-able by market/creator; under-mined vs. its size. Information/Governance (crowd beliefs about AI's trajectory). Play-money caps the stakes score. **Score: 19/25** (a4 b4 c3 d4 e4).

**Metaculus AI questions.** Reputation-stakes forecasting on AI progress, benchmarks, and governance, via an open API ([metaculus.com/api](https://www.metaculus.com/api/)). Distinct signal — calibrated expert-ish forecasts about AI. Honest caveat: **fairly claimed** — Rethink Priorities and multiple papers already mine Metaculus AI predictions, and Metaculus runs FutureEval on this very data (c≈2). **Score: 18/25** (a4 b4 c2 d4 e4).
