Revisiting frontier 9 (08-17 took AI shareholder resolutions, Manifold, Metaculus). Today's distinct AI angles: firms' own AI-risk disclosures, and the newest twist — AI agents becoming autonomous participants in markets and governance.

## Top find: Corporate AI-risk disclosures in 10-K filings (EDGAR)

Every US public company files an annual 10-K with a risk-factor section; a rapidly-growing share now names AI as a material risk. The filings are fully queryable via the free [EDGAR full-text-search API](https://efts.sec.gov/), turning them into a company × year × risk-type panel of how firms govern and frame AI. Distinct from the 08-17 shareholder-resolution find (that's investor proposals; this is management's own disclosures).

**AI nexus + agenda.** How firms disclose and govern their own AI exposure — **Governance** (corporate governance of AI), AI nexus intrinsic.

**Why it clears the bar.** Mandatory disclosures under SEC/legal stakes, not opinion (a); risk-factor exhaust accruing every fiscal year (b); panel-able by company × year × risk-type (d); on-agenda (e5). **Unclaimed — honestly (c≈2):** this is now a hot research area — [arXiv 2508.19313](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.19313), [Harvard corpgov](https://corpgov.law.harvard.edu/2025/10/15/ai-risk-disclosures-in-the-sp-500-reputation-cybersecurity-and-regulation/), a dedicated [AI Disclosures Project](https://ai-disclosures.org/research), and a released firm-year [Mendeley panel](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/nf88fc7f24/1) (~50–60 firms). The unmined edge is the **full 7,000-firm EDGAR panel** and the **disclosure gaps** (risk types firms systematically omit — environmental, labor displacement, info-ecosystem pollution).

**Evidence from a live sample.** Querying EDGAR today (10-K, "artificial intelligence"): **445 (2019) → 848 (2021) → 1,295 (2023) → 2,436 (2024) → 3,324 (2025) → 3,754 (2026)** — an ~8× rise; and **3,242 of the 2025 filings** name AI in a risk-factor context, so AI has moved from novelty to standard material risk. Corroborated: 380 S&P 500 firms (76%) expanded AI-risk language in 2025. Script + CSV in [`analysis/2026-08-27/`](../analysis/2026-08-27/SUMMARY.md). **Score: 22/25** (a5 b5 c2 d5 e5).

**Access & cost.** Free API + bulk 10-K text; a weekend for the mention panel, a few weeks to classify risk types and link to outcomes. **Nearest work / difference:** studies count mentions on ~50 firms; nobody has built the full-universe panel or modeled *which* AI risks firms hide vs. hype by industry. **Headline figure:** share of 10-Ks framing AI as risk vs. opportunity, by sector, 2019→2026.

**AI agents as autonomous market actors.** The newest frontier-9 twist: AI agents trading real money. AI-native decentralized governance — market-driven allocation of an AI network's rewards, plus a real on-chain voting record. Nascent and crypto-niche, data via chain explorers. Governance. **Score: 19/25** (a4 b4 c4 d4 e4).
