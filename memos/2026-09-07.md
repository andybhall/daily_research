## Top find: LLM Election Data 2024 (Cen et al., MIT)

An MIT team ([Cen, Ilyas, Driss, Park, Hopkins, Podimata, Madry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.18446); [CSAIL writeup](https://www.csail.mit.edu/news/peering-inside-political-ai-how-llms-responded-2024-election)) queried production chatbots with a fixed battery of election questions on a **near-daily cadence through the 2024 season** and released the raw run at [huggingface.co/datasets/sarahcen/llm-election-data-2024](https://huggingface.co/datasets/sarahcen/llm-election-data-2024) (MIT license). It is the thing the election-audit one-shots in our ledger (StatesUnited, CDT) are not: a **longitudinal panel** of model behavior, not a snapshot.

**AI nexus + agenda.** Bullseye **Information** — how deployed AI answered the exact voting, candidate, and election-integrity questions real users would ask, and how those answers drifted over an election. AI nexus intrinsic.

**Why it clears the bar.** Revealed behavior of production models under election stakes (a4); genuinely longitudinal, 112 near-daily snapshots per question (b3 — assembled, not incidental); **unclaimed** — a Google Scholar/web check surfaces only the authors' own paper plus press, no third-party reuse (c4); a textbook **panel** of model × question × day × partisan-framing (d5); dead-on agenda (e5). **Score: 21/25** (a4 b3 c4 d5 e5).

**Evidence from a live sample.** I pulled the full 92,000-file manifest and three sample question series via the HF API this run. Real numbers: **8 model configurations** (claude-3-5-sonnet, -haiku, -opus, sonnet-with-internet, gemini-1.0-pro ±internet, gpt-3.5-turbo, gpt-4-turbo), each with **12,638 question-runs** (**91,998** CSVs total); **605 distinct questions** across three conditions — `baseline` capability controls, `endo` voter-facing (election process, issues, candidates), and `exo` (predictions, exit polls, integrity). Each daily question holds **112 near-daily snapshots, 2024-07-23 → 2024-11-12**, in **19 fields** including a partisan pre-prompt (`"I am a Republican. {}"`) and placeholder variations (`{'state': 'South Carolina'}`) for steering tests. Artifacts + sample series in [`analysis/2026-09-07/`](../analysis/2026-09-07/SUMMARY.md).

**Access & cost.** Public HF repo, no auth; ~92k small CSVs, a few GB — a `huggingface-hub` snapshot and a weekend to reshape into a model×question×day panel. **Nearest work / difference:** the authors' own paper analyzes steering and implicit predictions; nobody else has touched the deposit, and the obvious external angles — cross-model convergence on contested claims, drift around debates and Election Day, partisan-framing gaps — are wide open. **Headline figure:** for a fixed election-integrity question, the share of the 8 models giving a "safe/accurate" answer, plotted daily July→November with debate and Election-Day markers.

- **ShareChat** ([arXiv 2512.17843](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.17843v2)) | ~19/25 | 142,808 real chatbot conversations (660k turns, 101 languages) scraped from publicly shared ChatGPT/Perplexity/Grok/Gemini/Claude URLs — genuine AI exhaust with timestamps. **Watch:** paper says "will be publicly available"; no DOI yet, so unverifiable today. Revisit on release.
- **Synthetic candidates & political parties** ([Zenodo 19521950](https://zenodo.org/records/19521950)) | ~17/25 | first catalog of AI-run parties/candidates that actually stood in elections (DK/FI/JP/SE/UK…). On-frontier and novel, but ~10 curated cases — not panel-able.
- **Microsoft Copilot election answers** ([Zenodo 10517697](https://zenodo.org/records/10517697/latest)) | ~14/25 | largest public DB of RAG chatbot responses to voter prompts (Swiss/Bavarian/Hesse 2023) — but a constructed audit and already worked by AlgorithmWatch.
- **LLM-generated parliamentary text detection** ([Zenodo 20611280](https://zenodo.org/records/20611280)) | reject | human vs. Gemini/GPT parliamentary text — a constructed detection benchmark, our OpenFake-genre kill.
- **AI in Everyday Life 2026** ([Zenodo 22091497](https://zenodo.org/records/22091497)) | reject | course survey of AI attitudes/literacy — stated preference, assembled to be studied.
