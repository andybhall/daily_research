# Dataset Hunt — 2026-10-20

**Frontier:** 3. Optimized persuasion text (ads, SMS/robotext, push notifications, email subject lines, A/B-tested fundraising copy)
**Verdict:** Null day — nothing cleared the bar.

Sixth pass on frontier 3 (08-11 Snap/FCC/TikTok ad libraries; 08-21 Talk2AI + Levers of Political Persuasion + AI election-info audits; 08-31 AI influence-ops + ads-in-AI-answers + Meta AI-labeled ads; 09-10 null; 09-30 null). The AI-persuasion corners are logged and the frontier was null on both prior visits. I probed a sub-territory earlier visits only flagged — AI-generated *email* copy and 2026 AI *robocalls* — and neither yields a deposited dataset.

The strongest lead is the AI layer the 09-30 email-archive candidate was missing — but it's unreleased. Kiran Garimella analyzed **587,682 political emails (Mar 2015–Feb 2026)** from the open [Archive of Political Emails](https://politicalemails.org/), running the Pangram AI-text detector on a 3,550-email sample to measure the rise of AI-written fundraising copy ([Garimella](https://kirangarimella.substack.com/p/ai-written-text-in-us-political-emails)). This confirms the AI nexus the raw archive lacks is real and measurable — but it's a Substack *analysis*, not a released labeled corpus: the archive itself carries no AI tags, the detector labels aren't deposited, the sample is small, and the author has already published the headline finding. The open corpus plus a detector is a build path, not a probe-able deposit — and the archive on its own is the same indirect-AI object I rejected on 09-30.

The 2026 campaign stories are loud but un-deposited. The FCC is weighing a petition to **loosen political-robocall rules, including AI-voice calls**, before the midterms ([WaPo](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/10/07/fcc-political-robocalls-artificial-intelligence-ai-midterms/d99a51ae-c25d-11f1-8170-681419af1cc9_story.html)), but there is **no open corpus of detected 2026 political voice-clone recordings** (the nearest open set, DeepSpeak-Agentic, is agent-detection, not political). And the Wesleyan Media Project now counts **$137M on AI-generated/enhanced midterm ads, most undisclosed** ([Phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2026-10-tracking-uncovers-million-spent-ai.html)) — a refreshed, larger version of the aggregates-only figure already rejected on 09-30, still with no open ad-level release.

*Meta-run flag:* eleventh straight null, all on 5th–6th-visit frontiers (last finds 10-02, 10-05). Same structural diagnosis; still flagging for review rather than editing the rotation unilaterally.
