# Dataset Hunt — 2026-10-01

**Frontier:** 4. Platform / API exhaust (app-store metadata, browser extensions, model marketplaces, ToS diffs, changelogs, review corpora)
**Verdict:** Null day — nothing cleared the bar.

Fifth pass on frontier 4 (08-12 HF Hub metadata / Civitai / Pixiv / DSA Transparency DB / Community Notes; 09-01 MCP registries / AI Agent Marketplace / AUP tracking; 09-11 model-card safety disclosures; 09-21 null). The marketplace-and-metadata corners are well-mined, and today's live candidates fail along the now-familiar seam: **the clean open archives are off the political agenda, and the on-agenda objects aren't released as open, probe-able datasets.** I screened five and promoted none — a null beats a padded find.

The one genuinely open, Willis-genre archive here is off-agenda. **AINativeLab/gptstore-data-backup** ([GitHub](https://github.com/AINativeLab/gptstore-data-backup)) is a real daily archive of OpenAI GPT Store trending data — 565+ commits, snapshots through 2026, name / rank / engagement / rating / category per GPT — but its categories are generic (Writing, Productivity, Programming, DALL·E) with **no political, campaign, or government tagging**, the same off-agenda wall that killed the Character.AI corpus on 09-21.

The on-agenda objects, meanwhile, don't convert to datasets. **TikTok's AIGC labels** are the largest synthetic-media exhaust anywhere — TikTok reports having labeled **more than 3 billion videos** as AI-generated as of July 2026 ([report](https://crossglobemarketing.com/blogs/social-media-ai-transparency-2026-tiktok-ai-labeling/)) — but that's an aggregate press figure, not an open per-video dataset to pull. The **Midas Project AI Policy Watchtower** ([watchtower](https://www.themidasproject.com/watchtower)) is squarely on-frontier (ToS/changelog diffs) and on-agenda (model governance and incident reporting) — it logs 60+ dated changes to RSPs, safety frameworks, usage policies, and voluntary commitments across Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI, Meta, and others since December 2022, tagged by change type and severity — but it's a **web-only, curated advocacy tracker** (~60 entries, no download/API/repo), not open incidental exhaust. And **C2PA content credentials** — which OpenAI now stamps on every ChatGPT image ([Content Credentials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Content_Credentials)) — is an open *standard*, not a corpus: there's no probe-able collection of manifests to analyze. The fresh **political-chatbot evaluation sets** (e.g., [gemmozero/ai-elections-2026](https://huggingface.co/datasets/gemmozero/ai-elections-2026); ISD's 2,400-prompt [Chatbots and the Ballot Box](https://www.isdglobal.org/publication/chatbots-and-the-ballot-box-evaluating-accuracy-sourcing-and-language-gaps-in-ai-answers-to-election-questions/)) are constructed-to-be-studied research data that collide with the AI election-info audits already logged (08-21).

*Pattern note (for the Sunday meta-run, not acted on here):* this is the fifth frontier in a row to come up null or near-null on a 4th–5th repeat visit (09-21 F4, 09-28 F1, 09-29 F2, 09-30 F3, today F4). The clean open AI-political datasets on the most-mined frontiers appear largely found; the rotation may be due for a refresh.
