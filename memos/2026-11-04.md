# Dataset Hunt — 2026-11-04

**Frontier:** 8. Labor & organizational (job postings, Glassdoor/Blind reviews, union filings, WARN notices, H-1B/LCA disclosures)
**Verdict:** Null day — nothing cleared the bar.

Sixth pass on frontier 8 (08-26 union AI clauses + NLRB + NYC LL144 + Worker Info Exchange; 09-15 AI-attributed layoffs; 09-25 AI/ML H-1B; 10-05 international CBA AI-clause database; 10-15 null). The on-agenda AI-labor objects are logged, and this frontier's political-agenda fit is the weakest in the rotation. I probed two fresh corners — federal-workforce AI cuts and workplace algorithmic-management disclosure — and neither is a probe-able open dataset.

The **federal-workforce AI** story is large but unmeasurable as an AI dataset. DOGE-era cuts removed ~9% of the federal workforce (≈238,000 net, per Pew) and agencies are pushing an "AI-first" automation strategy ([GovExec](https://www.govexec.com/technology/2026/04/federal-workforce-trauma-stumbling-block-ai-adoption/413139/); [FedSmith](https://www.fedsmith.com/2026/10/02/ai-could-change-federal-jobs/)), but no dataset separates AI-driven federal job losses from buyouts, deferred resignations, and attrition — Challenger's counts don't break out federal AI cuts, and analysts warn of "AI-washing" of layoffs ([The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/technology/5870898-ai-job-cuts-analysis-trump-admin/)). It's an economic story with a weak political-agenda nexus, overlapping the 09-15 AI-layoffs find.

The **workplace algorithmic-management disclosure** corner is all law and proposal, not data. Uruguay's **Law 20.396/2025** mandates disclosure of automated workplace monitoring and decision systems ([tandfonline](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/24750158.2026.2694614)); California's **AB 1898** would require 90 days' notice before deploying a "workplace AI tool" ([CA analysis](https://apcp.assembly.ca.gov/system/files/2026-03/ab-1898-schultz-apcp-analysis.pdf)); and an IRPP commentary proposes a **Canadian workplace AI registry** ([Policy Options](https://policyoptions.irpp.org/2026/06/workplace-ai-registry/)). These regimes would *generate* exactly the on-agenda dataset I want — a register of workplace algorithmic-management systems — but none has produced a public registry yet. The academic work (warehouse algorithmic management, surveillance perceptions) is interview/survey research, not open datasets.

*Meta-run note:* a normal null (last find 10-27). Like several recent frontiers, the live prospects here are regimes pending data release (workplace-AI disclosure registries under Uruguay's law, CA AB 1898, the Canadian proposal) rather than deposits in hand — added to the watch list alongside the EU AI Office enforcement register and the government-AI-chatbot FOIA corpus.
