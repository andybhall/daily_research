# Dataset Hunt — 2026-10-08

**Frontier:** 1. Legal / judicial exhaust (dockets, sanctions orders, amicus briefs, discovery exhibits, consent decrees, PACER/RECAP corners)
**Verdict:** Null day — nothing cleared the bar.

Sixth pass on frontier 1 (08-09 seeds, 08-19 AI Hallucination Cases DB, 09-08 Algorithmic Contestability DB / DAIL / BIPA, 09-18 ChatGPT JCCP mass tort / AI-discovery docs, 09-28 null). The clean open AI-legal datasets are logged, and this frontier was *already* null on its last visit. Today's candidates repeat the same four kill reasons — **commercial trackers repackaging public dockets, a paywalled vendor feed, an already-logged database, or a small enforcement set that's being dismantled.** I screened the frontier and promoted none — a null beats a padded find.

The trackers are the same wall I hit on 09-08 and 09-28. The **AI Lawsuit Tracker** ([ailawsuittracker.com](https://ailawsuittracker.com/cases/)) offers a free CSV of ~246 cases against 76 companies, and **regulations.ai** ([enforcement](https://regulations.ai/enforcement)) lists ~280 actions — but both are commercial repackagings of CourtListener/PACER dockets, the genre I've already rejected twice. The **gemmozero/ai-litigation-2026** Hugging Face set ([HF](https://huggingface.co/datasets/gemmozero/ai-litigation-2026)) is ~380 CourtListener rows behind a €29/month paywall with inconsistent CC-BY vs CC-BY-NC labeling — the same vendor whose election set I rejected on 10-01. And the **GW Database of AI Litigation** is the DAIL index already logged on 09-08, now in archive mode — not fresh.

The one genuinely on-agenda primary-source object doesn't clear either. **FTC AI enforcement consent decrees** — Rite Aid (a five-year facial-recognition ban), Evolv, NGL Labs, DoNotPay, Sitejabber, accessiBe — are real court-filed governance exhaust ([Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/08/a-look-at-the-ftcs-current-approach-to-ai-regulation)). But it's a **small set (~10 actions), not a deposited dataset** (you'd assemble it from FTC.gov press releases, which carry no AI tag), it's a subset of the state-AG/multi-regulator enforcement I rejected on 09-28, and — decisively — the corpus is **shrinking, not accruing**: the FTC vacated its 2024 Rytr consent order, and the administration's AI Action Plan directs the FTC to review and set aside AI orders that "unduly burden AI innovation" ([All About Advertising Law](https://www.allaboutadvertisinglaw.com/2026/01/the-ftc-walks-back-its-rytr-enforcement-action-signaling-a-shift-in-federal-ai-regulation.html)). An enforcement regime being dismantled is not a panel. The **International Deepfake Case Law Database** ([naturalandartificiallaw.com](https://naturalandartificiallaw.com/international-deepfake-case-law-database/)) is a narrow commercial tracker adjacent to the PDID (08-18) and the deepfake-evidence angle already screened (09-28).
