# Dataset Hunt — 2026-11-19

**Frontier:** 3. Optimized persuasion text — ads, SMS/robotext, push notifications, email subject lines, A/B-tested fundraising copy
**Verdict:** Null day — nothing cleared the bar.

Eighth pass on frontier 3 (08-11 persuasion seeds; 08-21 AI-election audits; 08-31 Levers of Political Persuasion / Talk2AI; 09-10, 09-30, 10-20, 10-30, 11-09 null). Sixth consecutive null here. The persuasion-text frontier's AI corners are comprehensively logged, and every fresh candidate this run collides with a logged Found or Reject, or is an unreleased vendor practice.

The 2026-midterm AI ad material is still aggregates-only. The Wesleyan Media Project's tracker now stands at **164–220 AI-generated ads / ~$80–137M**, 80% from Republican candidates/groups, with only 22–31% carrying an AI disclosure ([Fast Company](https://fastcompany.com/91618378/midterms-ai-generated-political-ads-spending-research); [aiweekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/wesleyan-80m-on-164-ai-generated-midterm-ads-80-gop)). But these are coder-flagged estimates against licensed AdImpact spend, not an ad-level open deposit — access is by email, and it's a heavily-claimed flagship. Already rejected (10-30, 11-09); no Zenodo/OSF ad-level release surfaced for the post-election window.

The AI campaign-texting exhaust exists but stays private. Vendors like **Vector Political** report **3M+ AI-sent messages → 35,000+ conversations** this cycle, with full transcripts delivered to clients and no disclosure requirement in most states ([CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/ai-campaign-texts-midterms/); [Michigan Public](https://www.michiganpublic.org/2026-07-12/getting-campaign-text-messages-ahead-of-midterms-there-could-be-an-ai-bot-behind-it)). One executive calls the approach "secret sauce" clients won't share — exactly the logged reject (AI-personalized political SMS/voice bots, no released corpus). The adjacent research corpus, **Lin et al.'s human–AI persuasion dialogues** (8,134 fact-checked AI statements across US/Canada/Poland races), withholds its transcripts as dual-use ([summary](https://fabiogiglietto.github.io/fg-zettelkasten/Papers/Lin2025-xp)) — the logged "Persuading voters" reject.

Everything else repeated known kills. **GPTarget2024** and the LLM-microtargeting/persuasion studies (Hackenburg 19-LLM × 707 issues; Aptima's 134,136 synthetic persuasion attacks) are constructed experiments — the Levers-of-Political-Persuasion Found (08-31) plus the logged LLM-persuasion-lab-cluster reject ([arXiv 2606.05961](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.05961)). **AI fundraising email subject-line A/B** returns only vendor marketing blogs (generic open-rate claims, no political corpus), not a dataset. No fresh push-notification, robotext, or ad-creative deposit appeared.

*Meta-run note:* sixth straight frontier-3 null (last find 11-18, World Bank AI procurement, frontier 2). The persuasion-text frontier is the most saturated in the ledger: the clean objects (political-email corpora, ad libraries, Levers/Talk2AI, AI-answer audits) are logged, and the live 2026 activity consolidates into aggregate trackers (WMP) and private campaign-texting vendors that don't deposit data. Same posture; flagging, not editing the rotation.
