The loud post-election markets story isn't about AI. Kalshi and Polymarket took **~$1.9B in midterm wagering** and priced Democrats to take the House (~94%) and Senate (~74%) ([Variety](https://variety.com/2026/digital/news/midterm-elections-odds-prediction-spending-house-and-senate-1236938339/); [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/02/prediction-market-traders-now-see-nearly-75percent-chance-of-democrats-winning-the-senate.html)) — a genuine markets-and-politics object, but the markets are about *election outcomes*, not AI, and no platform released an open post-election dataset. The AI-intrinsic slice here — AI agents trading these markets — was screened and rejected on 10-06 (vendor-estimated labels, no clean dataset).

The "AI governance" hits are the wrong kind of object. A Nov 4 AI Markets Group release and several analyst reports size the **AI-governance software market** (tools to approve/restrict/audit enterprise AI) at $0.35B–$12.5B by 2030 depending on the firm ([PRNewswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/aimg-sizes-ai-governance-platforms-market-at-12-5-billion-by-2030-microsoft-databricks-and-salesforce-top-ai-leadermap-302929575.html)) — commercial market-size forecasts, off this agenda and not datasets. The arXiv items (AI-agent forecasting on 4,076 markets; Polymarket "prediction laundering" governance) are research papers, not AI-in-politics exhaust. No DAO or proxy dataset surfaced beyond what's logged.

*Meta-run note:* fourth straight frontier-9 null; this frontier is tied with frontier 2 as the most exhausted in the rotation. As elsewhere, the live prospects are watch items pending release (EU AI Office enforcement register, workplace-AI disclosure registries, the government-AI-chatbot FOIA corpus), not new per-frontier deposits. Flagging, not editing the rotation.
