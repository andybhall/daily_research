# Dataset Hunt — 2026-10-09

**Frontier:** 2. Money trails (procurement, financial disclosures, Form 990s, lobbying registrations, grants, subawards, sole-source contracts)
**Verdict:** Null day — nothing cleared the bar.

Fifth pass on frontier 2 (08-10 FARA/electoral bonds; 08-20 USASpending AI procurement + AI lobbying + Open Phil philanthropy; 09-09 Leading the Future super PAC + data-center subsidies; 09-29 null). The clean open AI-money datasets are logged, and this frontier was already null on its last visit. Three-and-a-half weeks before the midterms the AI-political-money story is louder than ever — but louder is not the same as unclaimed. Today's candidates fail the one check this frontier keeps failing: **the data is maximally mined, the AI slice is already built, and the object is the other-half of a find already in the ledger.** I screened and promoted none — a null beats a padded find.

The headline development is real and on-agenda: the AI-regulation fight now has **two opposing super PACs**. Against the acceleration-side **Leading the Future** (logged 09-09), the safety side has stood up **Public First Action**, substantially funded by Anthropic ($20M committed) with a $1M personal gift from Dario Amodei ([Washington Examiner](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/policy/technology/4652035/anthropic-ceo-dario-amodei-donated-1-million-ai-regulation-super-pac/); [Transformer](https://www.transformernews.ai/p/ai-safetys-hard-money-secret-weapon-midterms-anthropic-openai)). And FEC itemized data reveals a sharp intra-industry donor cleavage — OpenAI employees gave ~$296K this cycle almost entirely to Democrats, while Anthropic staff gave ~$1.6M in Q2 (≈69% Democratic), at far higher participation rates ([SF Standard](https://sfstandard.com/2026/07/18/ai-s-new-political-donor-class-already-outspending-big-tech-s-last-one/)). Public Citizen puts crypto+AI+Big-Tech+betting super-PAC spending near $500M for the cycle ([Public Citizen](https://www.citizen.org/article/corporate-supremacist-super-pacs-500-million-midterm-crypto-ai-sports-betting/)).

But none of this is a hunt find. The **AI-industry employee/executive giving** object is built from FEC individual itemized contributions — the single most-mined dataset in American campaign finance — and the AI-employer slice specifically has *already* been pulled and published this cycle by multiple outlets ([SF Standard](https://sfstandard.com/2026/07/18/ai-s-new-political-donor-class-already-outspending-big-tech-s-last-one/); [Transformer](https://www.transformernews.ai/p/ai-safetys-hard-money-secret-weapon-midterms-anthropic-openai)), so it fails "unclaimed" at both the genre and slice level; its AI nexus is also only via employer. **Public First Action** is a genuinely distinct FEC committee, but it's the same object-type as the Leading the Future find (an AI super PAC's filings) — the other side of a money war I already logged on 09-09, an update rather than a new object, and committee-level FEC data is well-mined. Logging either would be padding the most-covered AI-money story of the cycle. (Q3 FEC reports, due mid-October, weren't available this run — but a refresh of logged committees wouldn't change the verdict.)
