## Top find: State AI legislation corpus (NCSL AI Legislation Database)

Every US state legislature is now writing AI law, and NCSL maintains a topic-tagged database of enacted and pending AI bills since 2024, updated monthly ([NCSL AI Legislation Database](https://www.ncsl.org/financial-services/artificial-intelligence-legislation-database); [MultiState 50-state tracker](https://www.multistate.ai/artificial-intelligence-ai-legislation)).

**AI nexus + agenda.** How AI is actually being governed in the US — bullseye **Governance** (AI lawmaking), AI nexus intrinsic.

**Why it clears the bar.** Legislators drafting binding AI rules under real stakes (a); bills accreting every session (b); panel-able by state × bill × topic × session × status (d); on-agenda (e5). **Unclaimed (c≈4):** law-firm and NCSL trackers count bills, but no rigorous academic panel of the full state-AI-law corpus (text, sponsors, diffusion, enactment) exists.

**Evidence — verified, partial probe.** Verified this run: states introduced **1,208 AI bills in 2025 — the first year all 50 states did — and enacted 145**; by mid-2026, **1,561 AI bills across 45 states, with 84 laws enacted in 27 states in Jan–Jun 2026** alone (already past 2025's full-year total). The hottest 2026 areas: generative-AI regulation, algorithmic accountability, AI hiring, and nonconsensual deepfakes. Honest caveat: figures are from the trackers; full bill text is pullable via LegiScan (API-key gated), so no clean live pull today. Logged in [`analysis/2026-09-02/`](../analysis/2026-09-02/SUMMARY.md). **Score: 22/25** (a4 b4 c4 d5 e5).

**Access & cost.** NCSL/MultiState listings + LegiScan bill text/sponsors; a few weeks to build the coded state × topic × outcome panel. **Nearest work / difference:** trackers tally bills; nobody has modeled policy *diffusion* (which states copy which model bills) or which AI-industry positions predict enactment. **Headline figure:** enacted AI laws by state and topic, 2024→2026, as a diffusion map.

## Runner-ups

**Federal Register AI rulemaking (probed).** Executive AI policymaking — rules, proposed rules, EOs, and notices — is fully queryable via the [Federal Register API](https://www.federalregister.gov/developers/documentation/api/v1). **Probed today:** documents mentioning "artificial intelligence" ran **57 (2019) → 309 (2024) → 182 (2025) → 224 (2026)** — the 2024→2025 drop tracks the change of administration/AI-EO regime; 2025 breaks into **30 final rules, 33 proposed rules, 28 presidential documents, 91 notices**. Panel-able by agency × type × year. Distinct from the Regulations.gov *comments* seed (this is the rules themselves). **Score: 21/25** (a4 b5 c3 d4 e5).

**Congressional AI hearings corpus.** 28 congressional hearings on AI have been catalogued and analyzed ([Chamber of Progress](https://progresschamber.org/insights/we-used-ai-to-analyze-congressional-hearings-on-ai-heres-what-we-learned/)), plus Schumer's nine [A.I. Insight Forums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A.I._Insight_forums) (150+ experts, closed-door, no transcripts) and public hearing transcripts. Legislative AI deliberation; on-agenda but adjacent to the C-QUERI congressional-hearing-Q&A seed, and the Forums lack transcripts. **Score: 19/25** (a3 b4 c3 d4 e5).
