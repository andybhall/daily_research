## Top find: Brazilian electoral deepfake jurisprudence corpus

A [Zenodo dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/22013413) (CC-BY, deposited 2026-08-19) compiles Brazilian electoral-court (TSE + regional TRE) decisions, resolutions, and laws on election deepfakes, 2023–2026, each coded by authoring body, document type, and central themes. It is the non-US mirror of our US/Canada deepfake finds — how Brazil's electoral justice system draws the line between a banned deepfake and protected political satire.

**AI nexus + agenda.** Bullseye **Governance/Information** — judicial governance of AI-generated political media, in a non-US, non-English system. AI nexus intrinsic; distinct from the US-centric PDID (08-18) and the Canadian-election measurement (08-28).

**Why it clears the bar.** Real court rulings and binding electoral norms under electoral stakes (a4); the jurisprudence accretes across the 2024 municipal and 2026 general cycles, though the corpus is curated (b3); **barely touched** — 47 downloads, a fresh 2026 undergraduate-research deposit, no secondary analysis (c3); panel-able by body × document-type × theme × year (d4); dead-on agenda and genuinely non-US (e5). **Score: 19/25** (a4 b3 c3 d4 e5).

**Evidence from a live sample.** I pulled the corpus CSV this run: **129 coded documents** — **47 Brazilian judicial precedents** (TSE plus regional courts TRE-PE, TRE-SP, TRE-GO, TRE-MA, TRE-MG…), 56 institutional/international reports, 16 electoral resolutions, and 10 laws. Year references peak in **2024 (59)**, then 2025 (26) and 2026 (21), tracking the election cycles; the dominant themes after "deepfakes" (111) are **free expression (22)**, artificial intelligence (13), electoral propaganda (12), and **political satire (6)** — the recurring legal defense. For context (from [reporting](https://www.riotimesonline.com/tse-deepfake-bolsonaro-ai-elections-2026/), not this dataset): of **591 deepfake decisions in the 2024 municipal elections, only ~20% recognized manipulation while 62% treated the content as satire**. Artifacts in [`analysis/2026-09-24/`](../analysis/2026-09-24/SUMMARY.md).

**Access & cost.** One CC-BY CSV (+ a variable dictionary and dashboard); each precedent links to the TSE/TRE consultation system, so the corpus can be extended by scraping the courts directly. A weekend to build a decision × court × outcome panel and test the satire-vs-manipulation split by court and year. **Nearest work / difference:** the depositor catalogues the documents; nobody has modeled *which* courts recognize manipulation vs. accept the satire defense, or how rulings shifted from 2024 to the 2026 general election under the new TSE deepfake ban. **Headline figure:** share of TSE/TRE decisions finding a banned deepfake vs. protected satire, by court and year.
