## Top find: Talk2AI — longitudinal human–AI persuasive conversations

A publicly-deposited dataset of **3,080 conversations / 30,800 turns** from **770 profiled Italian adults across four weekly sessions (Spring 2025)**, each conversing within-subject with one of GPT-4o, Claude 3.7 Sonnet, DeepSeek-V3, or Mistral Large on climate change, math anxiety, and health misinformation, with per-session opinion change, conviction stability, perceived-humanness, sociodemographics, and psychometrics ([arXiv 2604.04354](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04354); GitHub MassimoStel/Talk2AI).

**AI nexus + agenda.** How AI dialogue durably reshapes beliefs on contested (climate, health-misinfo) topics — **Information** (AI-optimized persuasion), AI nexus intrinsic.

**Why it clears the bar.** Real belief change from real AI dialogue (a); a genuine **within-subject longitudinal panel** — the same 770 people tracked over four weeks (d5); brand-new and unmined (c4). The discount: it is a recruited study, not incidental exhaust (b≈2).

**Evidence — could not probe.** The data lives on GitHub, and this environment blocks external GitHub (raw 404 on the data paths, codeload 403; likely Git-LFS). Figures above are verified from the arXiv paper this run, not a live pull — logged in [`analysis/2026-08-21/`](../analysis/2026-08-21/SUMMARY.md). **Nearest work / difference:** most AI-persuasion studies are one-shot; Talk2AI's repeated-measures design lets you model *persistence and decay* of AI-induced belief change per person. **Headline figure:** within-person opinion trajectory over four AI sessions, by model and topic. **Score: 19/25** (a4 b2 c4 d5 e4).

## Runner-ups

**The Levers of Political Persuasion (Science 2025).** The largest AI-*political*-persuasion corpus: **19 LLMs, 707 political issues, 76,977 responses from 42,357 people, 466,769 fact-checked LLM claims**, with persuasiveness driven more by post-training (+51%) and prompting (+27%) than by scale or personalization ([Science](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aea3884); public code + OSF pre-registration). Directly political and openly released. Honest caveat: a constructed experiment and a high-profile Science paper, so incidental/unclaimed both score low. **Score: 18/25** (a5 b2 c2 d3 e5).

**AI election-information audits.** Repeated audits of how AI assistants answer voter questions — States United (ChatGPT + Google AI Overview: 497 responses Sep–Oct 2025 → 402 Jan–Feb 2026, error 6.9%/8.2% → 0%; [report](https://statesunited.org/resources/ai-and-elections/)), Reuters Institute (2024 UK), CDT (disability + voting). AI as a political-information intermediary; panel-able by model × question × time. Caveat: underlying question–response data is largely unpublished. **Score: 18/25** (a3 b3 c3 d4 e5).
