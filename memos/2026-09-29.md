**Verdict:** Null day — nothing cleared the bar.

Fourth pass on frontier 2 (08-10 FARA/electoral bonds; 08-20 USASpending AI procurement, AI lobbying, AI-safety philanthropy; 09-09 Leading the Future super PAC, data-center subsidies). The clean, open AI-money datasets on this frontier are already logged — federal AI procurement, LDA and EU lobbying, the Open Philanthropy grants database, the super-PAC filings, the subsidy tracker. Today's fresh candidates all failed the same way: **no open dataset, or a subset of a trail already in the ledger.** I screened the live money-trail frontier and promoted none — a null beats a padded find.

The three biggest fresh stories don't convert to datasets. **Gulf sovereign-wealth money into US AI** — MGX's $100B vehicle and PIF's Humain taking stakes in OpenAI, Anthropic, and xAI — is squarely on-agenda (foreign ownership and influence over frontier AI), but it lives in announced deals and consultancy decks; CFIUS review is not public and there is no aggregated open dataset. The **AI-safety funding ecosystem beyond Open Philanthropy** (the Survival and Flourishing Fund, Longview, Jaan Tallinn's speculation grantors) is real and important, but it's the same genre as our logged Open Phil find and has no single open release. And the **frontier-AI vendors' federal contracts** (GSA OneGov's $1-then-token deals, the DoD's ~$200M awards to Anthropic/Google/OpenAI/xAI, the Anthropic-ban saga) are vivid — but they sit inside the USASpending AI-procurement slice we already hold.
