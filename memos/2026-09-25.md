## Top find: AI/ML H-1B labor market (DOL OFLC LCA disclosure data)

Every H-1B hire requires a Labor Condition Application, and DOL publishes them: employer, job title, SOC code, offered wage, and worksite, in quarterly bulk files back to 2008 ([DOL OFLC](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/foreign-labor/performance)). The AI/ML slice is a regulatory-exhaust panel of who imports AI talent, where, and at what price.

**AI nexus + agenda.** The AI industry's labor supply and the **concentration of AI talent** in a few firms — the economic edge of the agenda, governance-adjacent (AI-power concentration; the AI-talent immigration fight), which I'll flag honestly rather than overstate.

**Why it clears the bar.** Real filings under legal/immigration stakes — revealed hiring behavior (a4); LCA disclosures accrue quarterly since 2008, a byproduct of the visa process (b4); **partly claimed** — H-1B data is heavily mined in general and commercial trackers slice AI/ML, but no academic AI-talent-concentration panel (c3); rich panel by employer × role × wage × worksite × year (d4); AI-relevant but economic, not squarely political (e3). **Score: 18/25** (a4 b4 c3 d4 e3).

**Evidence from a live sample.** Verified this run from OFLC-derived reporting ([VisaPulse](https://visapulseusa.com/ai-jobs), sourced to DOL LCA data): **FY2025 saw 7,536 AI/ML LCA filings, up from 2,807 in FY2020 — +168%**; the **average AI/ML wage is $277K vs. $121K across all H-1B — a 131% premium**. The talent is concentrated: top sponsors are **Amazon (4,252 filings), Apple (2,115), Microsoft (1,989)**, LinkedIn, and Qualcomm; top titles are Machine Learning Engineer and Applied Scientist; **California alone has ~15,000 filings**. Artifacts in [`analysis/2026-09-25/`](../analysis/2026-09-25/SUMMARY.md). **Honest caveat:** I verified the AI-slice aggregate and confirmed the DOL bulk xlsx (FY2008–2025) as the open source, but did **not** compute the full panel from the raw files this run — that's the build step; some cited sponsor/title counts are cumulative, not FY2025-only.

**Access & cost.** Free — DOL quarterly LCA .xlsx (large; a few GB across years) plus record-layout PDFs; a weekend to build the employer × AI-role × wage × worksite × year panel by filtering SOC codes and AI/ML titles. **Nearest work / difference:** commercial trackers report aggregate AI/ML totals; nobody has built the open concentration panel — how the AI-talent market centralized in Big Tech and a few metros, or how wages track each model race. **Headline figure:** AI/ML H-1B filings and median wage by employer and metro, 2015→2025, against AI-capex waves.
