And the biggest **governance** development — a December 2025 executive order directing the SEC to revise or rescind Rule 14a-8 and to make proxy advisors register as investment advisers ([Paul Weiss](https://www.paulweiss.com/insights/client-memos/executive-order-targeting-iss-and-glass-lewis-impact-on-the-2026-proxy-season-and-beyond)) — is a regulatory story (and the 14a-8 dismantling I already logged on 10-06), not a dataset.
