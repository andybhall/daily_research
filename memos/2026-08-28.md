## Top find: Deepfakes in the 2025 Canadian Election (measurement)

A ACM Web Conference 2026 study (Livernoche, Arodi, Musulan, Yang, Salvail, Marceau Caron, Godbout, Rabbany) that measured AI-generated imagery across **X, Bluesky, and Reddit** during the 2025 Canadian federal election ([arXiv 2512.13915](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.13915); [ACM](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3774904.3792948)).

**AI nexus + agenda.** Real AI-generated political imagery circulating in a real election — **Information** (AI-distorted political information), AI nexus intrinsic; non-US.

**Why it (barely) clears the bar.** Deepfakes deployed in the wild under electoral stakes, not a lab set (a); measured across platforms in real time (b); panel-able by platform × partisanship × time (d); on-agenda (e5). **Unclaimed (c≈3):** fresh (2026) and non-US, though deepfake-prevalence measurement is an active area and it is genre-adjacent to the US-focused PDID logged 08-18.

**Evidence — verified, partial access.** The study analyzed **187,778 posts**; **5.9% of election-related images were deepfakes**, and right-leaning accounts shared them far more often (**9.2%** of their images flagged vs **3.9%** for left-leaning), with flagged content skewing defamatory/conspiratorial. The honest catch: the *released* artifact is the **constructed OpenFake** detector-training set (3M real + 1M synthetic images), not the in-the-wild election corpus, whose public deposit I could not confirm from the paper. Logged in [`analysis/2026-08-28/`](../analysis/2026-08-28/SUMMARY.md). **Score: 20/25** (a4 b4 c3 d4 e5).

**Access & cost.** Detector + OpenFake are open; the in-the-wild election corpus likely needs an author request. **Nearest work / difference:** PDID curates US political-deepfake *incidents*; this measures *prevalence and partisan asymmetry* in one non-US election — the unbuilt object is a multi-country, multi-election in-the-wild deepfake panel with partisanship. **Headline figure:** deepfake share of election imagery by platform and account lean, across successive national elections.
