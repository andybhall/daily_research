The browser-extension corner is real exhaust but off-agenda. The lone political app (ReachThePeople.ai) showed 34 users and no reviews — not a dataset.

The platform-policy corner duplicates logged finds. And the **GPT Store** political-persona corner remains the logged-reject genre: the arXiv safety audit of 782 Custom GPTs and the 722k-GPT crawl are claimed research sets (GPTZoo/Beetrove reject, 08-12) ([arXiv 2502.01436](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.01436v3)).
