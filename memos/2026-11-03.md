# Dataset Hunt — 2026-11-03

**Frontier:** 7. Non-US / non-English sources (EU transparency registers, Japan, India election tech, national open-data portals)
**Verdict:** Null day — nothing cleared the bar, but the EU AI Act's opening enforcement record is a strong watch.

Sixth pass on frontier 7 (08-25 China CAC + EU registers; 09-04 EU AI-lobbying / OECD.AI / GPAI; 09-14 Canada AIA registry; 09-24 Brazilian deepfake jurisprudence; 10-14 null). This frontier genuinely yields fresh non-US deposits, so I trawled two angles — a fresh non-US election-deepfake corpus and the EU AI Office's new enforcement record — and both come up short of a probe-able open dataset today.

The biggest prospect is the EU AI Act's enforcement machinery, which just switched on. Enforcement powers for the AI Office and national authorities took effect **2 August 2026** ([EC enforcement framework](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/enforcement-ai-act)), and the Office reports it has begun sending requests for information and has **received Article-73 serious-incident reports** ([EC news, Oct 2026](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/enforcement-ai-act)). A serious-incident register or an enforcement-decision register would be a bullseye Governance find (AI incident reporting is named on the agenda). But there is **no public register yet** — the only tallies are commercial trackers (one counts ~14 actions + 3 inquiries as of Oct 2) with conflicting, unverified figures (e.g., a widely-repeated but unsubstantiated "€47M in August fines"). Too early and not open — a watch, not a find.

The non-US election-deepfake trawl turned up nothing fresh beyond what's logged. For 2026 target countries (Japan, India, Indonesia, Mexico, Philippines, Africa) no open Zenodo/OSF deposit surfaced; the hits were the **Brazilian** electoral-deepfake corpus already logged (09-24), the tiny **synthetic candidates/parties** set (rejected 10-07), **US-2024** deepfake sets (gated; off-geography), and the unreliable **gemmozero/ai-elections-2026** vendor table ([HF](https://huggingface.co/datasets/gemmozero/ai-elections-2026)). The strong logged non-US objects (China CAC, Canada AIA, Brazil jurisprudence) remain the state of the art here.

*Meta-run note:* a normal null (last find 10-27, the Bangladesh AI-misinfo register). The watch list is now the live edge of this project — EU AI Act Art. 71 high-risk DB (2027), EU AI Office enforcement/serious-incident register (just opening), and the Stanford/USC government-AI-chatbot-logs FOIA corpus — each a likely clean find on release. Flagging, not editing the rotation.
