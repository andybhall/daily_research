# Dataset Hunt — 2026-08-25

**Frontier:** 7. Non-US / non-English sources (EU transparency registers, Japan, India election tech, national open-data portals)
**Verdict:** China's CAC generative-AI registry — the world's only comprehensive public catalogue of who is allowed to ship AI, ~5,800 filings deep and barely mined in English.

The AI-focus gate finds a distinctive non-US object: how *other* states govern AI. China's mandatory registry is the standout; Europe's public-sector AI registers and India's AI-in-elections trail behind on cleanliness.

## Top find: China CAC generative-AI / algorithm registry

Every public-facing generative-AI service in China — text, image, audio, video, multimodal — must register with the Cyberspace Administration (CAC) before deployment; the filings are published as the world's only comprehensive, publicly accessible generative-AI registry ([Gradient Flow](https://gradientflow.com/inside-chinas-ai-registry/); [Trivium](https://triviumchina.com/research/seeking-the-next-deepseek-what-chinas-generative-ai-registration-data-can-tell-us-about-chinas-ai-competitiveness/)).

**AI nexus + agenda.** A live map of how a major state licenses and controls AI — bullseye **Governance** (AI regulation/registration), non-US/non-English, AI nexus intrinsic.

**Why it clears the bar.** Companies filing under hard regulatory stakes — register or you cannot legally deploy (a); regulatory exhaust accreting monthly (b); panel-able by service × company × province × time (d); on-agenda (e5). **Unclaimed (c≈3):** China-watchers (Trivium, [Basilinna](https://www.basilinna.com/insights/seeking-the-next-deepseek-what-chinas-generative-ai-registration-data-can-tell-us-about-chinas-ai-competitiveness), Gradient Flow) work it and a China/UK comparison exists ([arXiv 2606.00035](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.00035)), but rigorous academic panel analysis of China's AI-industry structure is thin.

**Evidence — partial probe.** Each filing carries registration number, approval date, tool name, company, province, functional description, and business model (B2B/B2C). Scale verified this run: **3,739 tools from ~2,353 companies (Apr 2025) → 5,822 entries from 3,787 entities (Nov 2025)**, +250–300/month, including DeepSeek and Ernie. I could not pull the raw list: the portal API (`beian.cac.gov.cn/api/algorithmpublicity/list`) returns 200 but `errno:440 "please log in first"`, and `www.cac.gov.cn` was unreachable here — analysts work from CAC's periodic bulk Excel releases. Logged in [`analysis/2026-08-25/`](../analysis/2026-08-25/SUMMARY.md). **Score: 23/25** (a5 b5 c3 d5 e5).

**Access & cost.** Official Excel announcements (Chinese) + scrapes; a couple of weeks to build the translated panel. **Nearest work / difference:** analysts count filings and hunt "the next DeepSeek"; nobody has built the province × time × model-lineage panel of China's state-gated AI industry. **Headline figure:** monthly new generative-AI filings by province and modality, 2023→2026.

## Runner-ups

**European public-sector AI registers (beyond the NL register).** Germany's "marketplace of AI opportunities" held **1,305 registered AI projects** at extraction; France runs four public-algorithm repositories (incl. France Travail); the EU JRC maintains a Public Sector Tech database ([OECD state-of-the-art report](https://wp.oecd.ai/app/uploads/2025/05/algorithmic-transparency-in-the-public-sector.pdf)). A non-US algorithmic-government panel, complementary to the NL register (logged 08-13) — but fragmented across countries and maturities. **Score: 19/25** (a4 b4 c3 d4 e5).

**India AI-in-elections.** In the 2024 general election, parties spent ~**US$50M** on authorized AI-generated campaign content and ~40 campaigns used generative AI; Google's **Shakti** fact-checking collective (300+ journalists, 50+ newsrooms) found only ~2% of reviewed stories were deepfakes/AI ([TechPolicy.Press](https://www.techpolicy.press/indias-experiments-with-ai-in-the-2024-elections-the-good-the-bad-the-inbetween/)). Non-US AI-in-elections, but the evidence is scattered across fact-checks and reports, not a single deposit. **Score: 18/25** (a4 b3 c3 d3 e5).
