# Dataset Hunt — 2026-11-09

**Frontier:** 3. Optimized persuasion text (ads, SMS/robotext, push notifications, email subject lines, A/B-tested fundraising copy)
**Verdict:** Null day — nothing cleared the bar.

Eighth pass on frontier 3 (08-11 Snap/FCC/TikTok ad libraries; 08-21 Talk2AI + Levers of Political Persuasion + AI election-info audits; 08-31 AI influence-ops + ads-in-AI-answers + Meta AI-labeled ads; 09-10, 09-30, 10-20, 10-30 null). Fifth consecutive null here. Now that the midterms are over the AI-ad numbers are final, but they're still aggregates, and no open AI-generated-persuasion corpus was released.

The Wesleyan Media Project closed the cycle at **220 AI-made political ads across 34 states, >$137M in spending, only 27% with a disclaimer** ([Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/137-million-has-been-spent-on-ai-generated-ads-ahead-of-midterms-only-27-included-a-disclaimer-2000827138)) — but WMP publishes only tracking aggregates, and its releases page shows no downloadable AI-ad dataset (the object rejected on 09-30, 10-20, and 10-30; no CREATIVE-2026 cross-platform release confirmed). CNN's AdImpact read ($16M disclosed vs ≥$38M undisclosed AI imagery) is likewise an aggregate ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/02/politics/ai-campaign-ads-disclosure-invs-vis)).

The fresh deposits are the wrong object. **SM-dot/consumer-sentiment-2026-midterms** ([GitHub](https://github.com/SM-dot/consumer-sentiment-2026-midterms)) is an AI-*coded* media/sentiment corpus (news and social sentiment with ad/bot/foreign flags), not AI-*generated* persuasion messages — off this frontier. The "synthetic political discourse" US-midterms set is a single-paper constructed comparison, and "The Levers of Political Persuasion with Conversational AI" is the persuasion-experiment work already logged (08-21). The raw Meta/Google ad libraries remain open but turning them into an AI-generated-persuasion dataset is still a label-it-yourself build, not a deposit.

*Meta-run note:* fifth straight frontier-3 null (yesterday's 11-06 catch-up find was frontier 10, Voter Prompts). The AI-persuasion-text corners are comprehensively logged or structurally un-releasable (aggregates, dual-use withheld transcripts, build-your-own ad-library labeling). Same diagnosis; flagging, not editing the rotation.
