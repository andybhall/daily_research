## Top find: Indian Parliament proceedings — raw dataset (Lok Sabha + Rajya Sabha)

A raw deposit of **questions, debates, and special mentions by all sitting MPs, current as of 31 Dec 2025**, with rich per-record metadata: MP code, party, constituency, ministry addressed, question type (starred/unstarred), date, and full question text ([Zenodo record 18146342](https://zenodo.org/records/18146342)).

**Why it clears the bar.** Parliamentary questions are behaviorally revealed under real stakes — an MP *choosing* which ministry to interrogate is position-taking and constituency service you can observe, not a survey answer. The corpus accumulates incidentally and is panel-able: the same MPs, parties, and ministries recur across sessions, so within-MP and within-constituency designs are available. It maps cleanly to **representation** (how elected voices translate into oversight).

**Unclaimed — honestly.** The Question Hour is *not* virgin territory: TCPD-IPD covers 1999–2019, an Oxford *Foreign Policy Analysis* paper hand-coded ~10k questions (2004–2019) on defense/foreign policy, and there's NLP topic-modeling work ([arXiv 2304.00235](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.00235)). But that work is issue-narrow, NLP-flavored, and stops at 2019. Nobody has exploited **this** deposit: full through-2025 coverage, *both* houses, plus constituency/party linkage as an accountability panel.

**Access & cost.** Open on Zenodo — direct download, no auth, standard open license (verify the exact CC terms on the record). Structured files keyed by `mpCode`. Cost is a weekend of joins/cleaning, not collection. The one risk: raw scrape fidelity (encoding, missing sessions) needs a validation pass.

**Nearest paper / our difference.** TCPD-IPD and the FPA paper ask *what topics* MPs raise. Our angle is *representational incentives*: do electorally vulnerable MPs shift toward constituency-service questions? Do opposition vs. ruling-party MPs target different ministries, and does that move with the electoral calendar?

**Headline figure.** A 25-year panel of question volume and ministry-targeting by party, annotated with the 2014 and 2019 government transitions — showing oversight intensity collapsing or surging with who holds power.

**First analysis.** Panel/RD on MP electoral margin vs. question activity: does surviving a close race change how much, and about what, an MP asks?

## Runner-up: C-QUERI — Congressional Questions, Exchanges, and Responses

A new dataset that segments congressional committee-hearing transcripts into utterances, tags question vs. answer, and **pairs each question with its response** ([arXiv 2509.21548](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.21548)). The paired-exchange structure is the novel part — most hearing corpora give you speeches, not the adversarial Q→A dyad. Behaviorally revealed (elite exchange under pressure), panel-able (members/committees recur), governance layer. Adjacent to our seeded *hearing-witnesses* entry but a distinct object (the dialogue, not the witness roster). Deposit location and exact coverage need direct verification (blocked this run).
