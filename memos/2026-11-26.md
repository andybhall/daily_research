# Dataset Hunt — 2026-11-26

**Frontier:** 10. Wildcard — academic dataverse trawl (ICPSR / Harvard Dataverse / Zenodo / OSF / Mendeley — recent deposits nobody has exploited yet)
**Verdict:** Null day — nothing cleared the bar.

Tenth pass on frontier 10 (08-08 EU consultations; 08-18 PDID; 08-28 Canadian deepfakes; 09-07 MIT LLM-election panel; 09-17 Grok/Perplexity fact-check requests; 10-07 null; 10-27 Bangladesh register [find]; 11-06 Voter Prompts [find]; 11-16 null). This is the frontier that still refreshes — but today's trawl, across Zenodo/Mendeley/ICPSR/OSF/Dataverse/HF, surfaced only the recurring traps: constructed LLM audits, media content analyses, classifier benchmarks, restricted deposits, and objects already in the ledger.

The one fresh real-world object is a refresh of a logged find. A Purdue team's **2026-midterm AI-political-post database** (~482 analyzed fully/partially synthetic posts; Trump in 279 of 482 = 58%) is the 2026 extension of the **Political Deepfakes Incident Database** (GRAIL Lab / Purdue), already logged (08-18) ([CBS Austin](https://cbsaustin.com/news/nation-world/more-political-deepfakes-muddying-the-waters-this-election-trump-most-frequent-target-purdue-political-deepfakes-incidents-database)) — same curator, same object, grown. Not a new deposit. The other deepfake material is logged or rejected: the USPED 231-item Zenodo set is restricted (reject), the Canadian 2025 arXiv corpus is logged, and Surfshark's cross-country counts are the rejected news-aggregate.

Everything genuinely new is constructed, claimed, or off-object. **ShareChat** (142,808 in-the-wild chatbot conversations, HF) is the already-rejected general corpus (11-06) — not political-specific. **ISD "Chatbots and the Ballot Box"** (6 LLMs × 2,400 election prompts) ([ISD](https://www.isdglobal.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Chatbots-and-the-Ballot-Box-US-elections.pdf)) and **VoteGEO-Bench** (generative-engine-optimization electoral visibility) ([arXiv 2610.21330](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.21330)) are constructed AI-election-answer audits — the logged genre (08-21 / MIT panel 09-07). Two fresh Zenodo deposits are constructed/replication: a voting-threshold study of **committees of 28 AI agents** (reasoning traces, not human voters) ([Zenodo 21637349](https://zenodo.org/records/21637349)) and an Oslo **content analysis of AI-election media coverage** (NYT + Chosun Ilbo, 2024) ([Zenodo 20281727](https://zenodo.org/records/20281727)). **PolyglotFakeFacts** (18-language fake/real news) is a classifier benchmark, not AI-generated-content exhaust ([Mendeley](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/gff8bmr4ff)). The **Tilburg conversational-VAA** field study is a constructed experiment behind restricted access (DataverseNL, OpenAccess false) ([Tilburg](https://research.tilburguniversity.edu/en/datasets/in-pursuit-of-informed-voters-three-experimental-studies-on-enhan/)). ICPSR 39673 (survey vote-share) has no AI nexus.

*Meta-run note:* third frontier-10 null (10-07, 11-16, 11-26), interspersed with the two finds that broke this autumn's streak (Bangladesh 10-27, Voter Prompts 11-06). The frontier still has the best odds, but today it offered only the constructed/claimed/restricted deposits it periodically throws up rather than the real-world exhaust (real voters' chats, real fact-check registers) that cleared. Same posture; flagging, not editing the rotation.
