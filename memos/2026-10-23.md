The strong hobbyist AI hoards are logged, and today every fresh candidate is either the **LLM-political-answer-monitoring genre already in the ledger** or a **media product, not a collector's hoard.**

The biggest new object is both. Time built a **publicly available database of 16M+ responses** from a dozen Anthropic/OpenAI/Google/Perplexity models to election questions, collected since July 2024 ([WaPo-indexed search]; Time's election-AI database). It's large and longitudinal — but it's the same object-type as the MIT LLM-election panel logged on 09-07 (how major models answer US-election questions over time) and TrackingAI (08-24), so logging it is mode collapse; and it's a newsroom product, not the individual-collector hoard this frontier targets. No genuinely new hobbyist hoard surfaced: the political-AI corners of this frontier (jailbreaks, refusals, system prompts, political-bias/answer tracking, AI-slop cleanup, decensored weights) are comprehensively logged, and the live activity now consolidates into media and academic products rather than quiet individual collections.
