Still the frontier's cleanest future Governance find on publication — nothing to pull today (watch unchanged since 11-03/11-13).

The fresh non-US fact-check corpus duplicates a logged object. The **Full Fact / University of Westminster** register of **112 UK AI-generated-misinformation items** (1 Jan 2025–31 Mar 2026, 63% "politics," coded for topic/format/harm/platform) is real but is a purposive register assembled for — and already analysed in — Full Fact's own report, the page states no download link, repository, or license for the underlying items, and it is the same coded-AI-misinformation-register genre already logged (Bangladesh, 10-27) ([Full Fact](https://fullfact.org/policy/reports/ai-generated-misinformation-in-the-uk-harm-context-and-classification/)). Small, cross-sectional, claimed, and access-unverified — ~13/25. The other non-US election material is logged or rejected: the Bangladesh Mendeley register ([h864gr3dyt](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/h864gr3dyt/1)) is the 10-27 Found; Surfshark's 235-incident / 46-country chart is the rejected news-aggregate (11-16); the Canadian 2025 corpus is logged.

China's content-labeling activity is a refresh, not a new object. The CAC runs the **deep-synthesis** registry alongside the **generative-AI** registry (a third-party monitor counts ~1,843 gen-AI filings as of 31 Aug 2026), and 2026 brought labeling-enforcement campaigns (Jianying/Maoxiang/Jimeng investigations; 5.6M items scrubbed) ([LessWrong monitor](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/TKcjrvcMuY7Ca7Ya9/china-ai-safety-regulation-monthly-monitor-issue-1-september); [DigitalPolicyAlert](https://digitalpolicyalert.org/event/37781-cyberspace-administration-published-batch-of-typical-cases-regarding-investigations-into-online-platforms-over-allegations-of-failure-to-label-ai-generated-content)). But the registries are the logged China CAC find (08-25), and the enforcement is governance news, not a deposited dataset.

The cross-jurisdiction regulation tracker overlaps a logged database. **Digital Policy Alert** is a structured global record of digital-policy changes and enforcement events (~5,600 events / ~3,000 changes, AI a named policy area, REST API + download) ([DPA](https://digitalpolicyalert.org/ai-governance)) — genuinely useful, but a curated regulatory-event tracker (assembled to be studied), strongly adjacent to the logged OECD.AI national-policy database (09-04) and the rejected commercial AI-law trackers. Not fresh incidental exhaust.
