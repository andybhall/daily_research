# Dataset Hunt — 2026-10-12

**Frontier:** 5. Institutional records (hearing transcripts, public comments, agendas, visitor logs, staffing/payroll, calendars)
**Verdict:** Null day — nothing cleared the bar.

Fifth pass on frontier 5 (08-13 Federal AI Use Case Inventory + NL/UK registers; 09-02 NCSL bills + Federal Register rulemaking + Congressional hearings; 09-22 GAO oversight; 10-02 public comments on AI rulemakings). Four legs of federal AI governance are logged — the *systems*, the *laws*, the *auditor*, and the *public's input*. The remaining institutional-record corners all failed today, each in a familiar way: **unreleased, thin, or dispersed.** I screened the frontier and promoted none — a null beats a padded find.

The **access** corners aren't released as data. Despite a visible 2026 influence story — a Sept 29 White House AI lunch with 31 tech leaders ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/29/trump-ai-meeting-list-ceos-johnson)), the AI-czar handoff from David Sacks to Jay Clayton ([WaPo](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/10/02/trump-expected-name-intelligence-chief-jay-clayton-new-ai-czar/)) — there is **no FOIA release or public dataset of White House visitor logs or the AI czar's calendar**; the lunch list is one-off journalism, not a feed. The **staffing** corner is just as thin: there is **no official 2026 roster of federal Chief AI Officers and no published CAIO Council membership list** — coverage is partial agency-by-agency reporting ([MeriTalk](https://www.meritalk.com/articles/meet-the-expanded-roster-of-federal-agency-caios/); [GovCIO](https://govciomedia.com/the-caios-leading-responsible-ai-development-across-government/)), not a deposited dataset.

The **state/local inventory** corner — the most promising distinct object, since 08-13 only covered the *federal* inventory — doesn't convert either. California's statutory **AB 302 high-risk automated-decision-system inventory** is real and recurring, but it's a thin, self-reported annual PDF: the Program Year 2024 report (Jan 2025) infamously **found zero high-risk ADS in use** across state agencies, and the Program Year 2025 report surveyed 182 agencies ([CDT](https://www.cdt.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/High-Risk-ADS-Report-for-Program-Year-2025.pdf)) but is published as an image-based PDF I couldn't parse this run — and critics already call the self-reported disclosure an undercount ([FAS](https://fas.org/publication/cali-ai-governance-state-implementation/)). California's separate GenAI use inventory (SAM 4986.3) is submitted to CDT **on request, not published**. And the **GovAI Coalition** (~900 member agencies) offers policy templates and a dispersed AI "registry"/FactSheet process, not an aggregated open dataset ([San José](https://www.sanjoseca.gov/your-government/departments-offices/information-technology/artificial-intelligence-inventory/govai-coalition/become-a-member/become-a-member)). The U.S. State Department's 2026 inventory CSV is a component of the federal inventory already logged (08-13).

*Pattern note (for the Sunday meta-run):* today is the fifth straight null (10-06 F9, 10-07 F10, 10-08 F1, 10-09 F2, 10-12 F5), all on 5th–6th-visit frontiers — though 10-02 (F5) and 10-05 (F8) were genuine finds on the same rotation, so the frontiers aren't dead, just picked over on short revisit cycles. Flagging, not acting; the rotation is the meta-run's call.
