# Dataset Hunt — 2026-08-12

**Frontier:** 4. Platform / API exhaust (app-store metadata, browser extensions, model marketplaces, ToS diffs, changelogs, review corpora)
**Verdict:** The Hugging Face Hub's own metadata — the living exhaust of the open-model supply chain, ~205 new public models an hour, barely mined as a behavioral panel.

First AI-focused run: every candidate below is gated on a clear AI nexus (HARNESS rule 6). This frontier is AI-native, so the constraint bit less than it will elsewhere.

## Top find: Hugging Face Hub metadata exhaust

The Hub's public API exposes, for every public model/dataset/Space, a rich record — downloads, likes, author, `createdAt`/`lastModified`, `pipeline_tag`, `library_name`, `gated` flag, license tags, and file lists ([Hub API docs](https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/en/api)). This is the incidental record of who builds, licenses, and adopts open AI, accumulating continuously.

**AI nexus + agenda.** This *is* the AI supply side — how models are produced, licensed, and governed. Maps to **Information** (the production of AI systems that generate political content) and **Governance** (open-model licensing and access control). AI nexus is intrinsic, not bolted on.

**Why it clears the bar.** Downloads/likes are revealed adoption under real stakes (a); the metadata piles up incidentally (b); models and authors recur, with download/like trajectories over time (d, panel-able). **Unclaimed (c≈3):** model-card and "model proliferation" studies exist ([model-card metadata, 2025](https://www.scitepress.org/Papers/2025/135718/135718.pdf)), but the Hub as a *behavioral panel* — adoption trajectories, author concentration, license drift, fine-tune lineage — is under-worked.

**Evidence from a live sample.** I pulled the top-1000 models by downloads plus the 500 newest today and analyzed them: downloads run from **240,250,376 down to a ~397k p10** (median 802,470); **518/1000 are Apache-2.0**, 185 MIT; tasks are dominated by text-generation (233) and image-text-to-text (157). Supply is **corporately concentrated** — 319 distinct authors, led by Qwen (117 of the top models), Google (44), Nvidia (37), Microsoft (36), Meta (35). And the 500 newest public models were created inside a ~2.4-hour window → **~205 new public models per hour**. Script + breakdowns in [`analysis/2026-08-12/`](../analysis/2026-08-12/SUMMARY.md).

**Access & cost.** Free JSON API, no auth, `huggingface_hub` client; rate-limited but bulk-friendly — a weekend to snapshot, a standing cron to build the time series nobody has. **Nearest work / difference:** prior studies snapshot documentation quality; nobody tracks the *adoption-and-governance panel* (who wins downloads, how licenses shift, how fast corporate labs crowd the leaderboard). **Headline figure:** monthly share of top-model downloads by corporate vs. community authors, 2022→2026. **Score: 22/25** (a4 b5 c3 d5 e5).

## Runner-ups

**DSA Transparency Database.** Every content-moderation "statement of reasons" filed by 700+ platforms under the EU DSA — ~38M *per day*, with fields flagging automated detection/decision ([download page](https://transparency.dsa.ec.europa.eu/explore-data/download)). The canonical dataset for **AI's role in governing information** (automated moderation at scale). Honest caveat: **not unclaimed** — the headline "most decisions are automated" finding is already published on 156M- and 439M-SoR samples ([arXiv 2312.04431](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.04431)), so c≈2; our only edge is a narrower unworked cross-section (e.g., automation-rate divergence across languages/countries over time). **Score: 22/25** (a5 b5 c2 d5 e5).

**X Community Notes public data.** Full notes + ratings, daily bulk since Jan 2021 ([repo](https://github.com/twitter/communitynotes)); AI nexus is the bridging-based ranking algorithm plus X's 2025 LLM-written "AI Note Writers." Panel-able by contributor/note; on-frontier platform exhaust. Claimed-ish — a comprehensive 4-year analysis exists ([Zenodo 16761304](https://zenodo.org/records/16761304)) — so c≈3. **Score: 20/25** (a4 b5 c3 d4 e4).

## Shortlist

- **GPTZoo / Beetrove — GPT Store custom-GPT metadata** ([GPTZoo](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.15630), 730k GPTs) → Rejected: released research datasets already exist.
- **AI Incident Database** ([OECD mirror](https://oecd.ai/en/catalogue/tools/ai-incident-database), ~800 incidents) → Rejected: curated (assembled to study), not incidental exhaust, and heavily used.

- **AI companion app reviews** (Replika/Character.AI, App Store) — AI-native user reactions, but agenda fit is weak (not political).
- **Hugging Face *datasets* + Spaces metadata** — same feed as the top find, a natural extension rather than a separate object.
