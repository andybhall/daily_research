## Top find: SpeechMap.ai

An independent project (by "xlr8harder") that puts the same battery of controversial requests — political argument, satire, religion, history, rights advocacy — to hundreds of models and records whether each answers, hedges, or refuses ([speechmap.ai](https://speechmap.ai/)).

**AI nexus + agenda.** A direct measure of how AI systems govern political expression — bullseye **Governance/Information** (AI as gatekeeper of political speech), AI nexus intrinsic.

**Why it clears the bar.** Real model refusal/compliance behavior, not stated policy (a); evaluations accreting continuously as models ship (b); a genuine panel of model × topic × time (d); on-agenda (e5). **Unclaimed (c≈4):** cited by Meta's Oversight Board and covered in tech press, but academic exploitation as a longitudinal free-speech panel is thin.

**Evidence from a live sample.** The site reports **375 models** and **795,000 responses analyzed**, **March 2023 → August 2026**, with four outcome codes (Complete / Evasive / Denial / Error). Per-topic asymmetries are already visible: "argue for traditional gender roles" draws 61% compliance vs **92.6%** with genders reversed; "outlaw Judaism" 10.5% vs "outlaw witchcraft" 68.5%. Partial probe only — the site exposes structured per-model/per-topic results, but bulk data needs a direct request (no public API); logged in [`analysis/2026-08-24/`](../analysis/2026-08-24/SUMMARY.md). **Score: 22/25** (a4 b4 c4 d5 e5).

**Access & cost.** Browsable now; a clean panel needs the maintainer's data or a scrape — a weekend to a couple of weeks. **Nearest work / difference:** press covers snapshots; nobody has modeled how each lab's refusal frontier *moves* across model versions and political topics over three years. **Headline figure:** per-lab compliance rate on political speech, by topic, 2023→2026.

## Runner-ups

**Leaked system-prompt archives.** A fan-curated hoard of the hidden system prompts configuring frontier AI ([leaked-system-prompts.com](https://leaked-system-prompts.com/); GitHub [YeeKal](https://github.com/YeeKal/leaked-system-prompts), sibling [CL4R1T4S](https://github.com/elder-plinius/CL4R1T4S)). **Cloned and analyzed today** (metadata only): **116 prompt files across 20+ vendors** — Anthropic (24), OpenAI (18), xAI (13), Perplexity/Google (6) — with a version panel by year (2022: 3 → 2024: 36 → **2025: 49** → 2026: 10). The hidden "constitutions" that encode each model's behavioral and political guardrails; distinct from the 08-14 L1B3RT4S jailbreak find (this is the config side). Governance. **Score: 21/25** (a4 b4 c4 d4 e5).

**TrackingAI.org.** Maxim Lott's independent tracker re-runs the Political Compass (and IQ tests) against ~19+ LLMs as they update, charting political-bias drift over time ([trackingai.org](https://www.trackingai.org/home)). A hobbyist longitudinal panel of AI political orientation. Honest caveat: the underlying idea is David Rozado's published LLM-political-preference work, so c is lower. **Score: 20/25** (a4 b4 c3 d4 e5).
