# Dataset Hunt — 2026-10-06

**Frontier:** 9. Markets & governance (prediction markets, DAO votes, proxy voting, shareholder resolutions, resolution disputes)
**Verdict:** Null day — nothing cleared the bar.

Fourth pass on frontier 9 (08-17 AI shareholder resolutions + proxy votes, Manifold, Metaculus; 08-27 10-K AI-risk disclosures + AI market-agents + Bittensor; 09-16 board AI-oversight proxy disclosures). The corporate-governance leg is now thoroughly mined — and its regulatory substrate is being removed. Today's candidates fail the usual seam: **the on-agenda objects are already logged or no longer accruing, and the genuinely fresh object has no clean, labeled, open dataset.** I screened the frontier and promoted none — a null beats a padded find.

Two corporate-governance angles collapsed on contact. **SEC no-action letters on AI shareholder proposals** (the resolution-disputes corner) looked promising, but the SEC's Division of Corporation Finance **permanently stopped responding to Rule 14a-8 no-action requests** (announced Aug 14, 2026, making permanent a Nov 2025 pause) and on Aug 28, 2026 sent OMB a proposed rule to **rescind Rule 14a-8 entirely** ([MoFo](https://www.mofo.com/resources/insights/251118-sec-staff-suspends-no-action); [Skadden](https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2026/08/sec-staff-withdraws-from-the-shareholder-proposal-process)) — so no AI-exclusion-dispute dataset is accruing behind it. And **anti-ESG / AI shareholder proposals** are a real 2026 story — AI landed on Alphabet, Meta, IBM, and Walmart ballots (IBM's failed at 2.4%), while anti-ESG proposals were the most-filed E&S type yet averaged ~1.7% support and none passed ([Harvard corpgov](https://corpgov.law.harvard.edu/2026/09/04/2026-proxy-season-trending-proposal-topics/); [Boardroom Alpha](https://www.boardroomalpha.com/2026-proxy-season-voting-scorecard/)) — but that's the shareholder-resolutions object already logged (08-17), and the vote data lives in commercial proxy databases (ISS, Boardroom Alpha).

The genuinely fresh object is striking but doesn't convert. **AI agents now trade political prediction markets at scale** — CoinDesk reports more than 30% of Polymarket wallets using AI agents, with ~37% of agents profitable versus 7–13% of humans, and agents like Olas's Polystrat running 4,200+ trades in a month ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/03/15/ai-agents-are-quietly-rewriting-prediction-market-trading)). The market data itself is wide open — Polymarket is on-chain, with official APIs, tick-level history, and Dune dashboards ([Awesome Prediction Market Tools](https://github.com/aarora4/Awesome-Prediction-Market-Tools)). But the clean, open layer (prices, trades, wallets) **isn't intrinsically about AI**, and the intrinsically-AI layer — *which* wallets are AI agents, and their P&L — **isn't a clean labeled dataset**: the 30% figure is a vendor estimate (LayerHub; "data shared by the team"), and identifying agent wallets on-chain is contested heuristics, not a deposited label. It's also adjacent to the 08-27 AI-market-agents/Bittensor note, and its tie to the political agenda (AI bots shaping the market-implied election odds that media report) is real but a step removed. A research project with a labeling problem, not a probe-able find.
