# Dataset Hunt — 2026-10-30

**Frontier:** 3. Optimized persuasion text (ads, SMS/robotext, push notifications, email subject lines, A/B-tested fundraising copy)
**Verdict:** Null day — nothing cleared the bar.

Seventh pass on frontier 3 (08-11 Snap/FCC/TikTok ad libraries; 08-21 Talk2AI + Levers of Political Persuasion + AI election-info audits; 08-31 AI influence-ops + ads-in-AI-answers + Meta AI-labeled ads; 09-10 null; 09-30 null; 10-20 null). Fourth consecutive null here. Four days before the midterms the AI-persuasion story is saturating coverage, but — as on every prior visit — it doesn't convert to an open, AI-labeled corpus.

The one fresh, open artifact is off-frontier. Pew Research (Oct 29, 2026) analyzed **20,751 Facebook/Instagram ads about data centers** using the Meta Ad Library API and a classifier, with a CSV download ([Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2026/10/29/on-facebook-and-instagram-political-ads-are-increasingly-critical-of-data-centers/)). It's a genuinely interesting political-information signal — the AI build-out's data centers are now a backlash *theme* in political advertising — but these are human-made ads that mention AI infrastructure, not AI-generated/optimized persuasion (this frontier's object), it's Pew's topic-level analysis rather than a released creatives corpus, and it's closer to the data-center-subsidies find (09-09) than to persuasion text.

Everything else repeated known kills. The **Wesleyan Media Project**'s AI-ad tracking is now ~$80M across ~170 AI ads ([MinnPost](https://www.minnpost.com/community-voices/2026/10/weve-tracked-80m-of-spending-on-almost-170-ai-generated-political-ads-this-year-its-weird/)) but publishes only aggregates — no ad-level release (rejected 09-30, 10-20); its 2022 CREATIVE cross-platform dataset has no confirmed 2026 equivalent yet. **gemmozero/ai-elections-2026** remains a thin, undocumented vendor table ([HF](https://huggingface.co/datasets/gemmozero/ai-elections-2026)). **AgoraSpeech** is Greek election *speeches* with ChatGPT annotations (not AI-generated persuasion), and the **Nature human–AI persuasion-dialogue** replication data withholds the raw transcripts (rejected 10-07). The raw Meta (390k+ ads since Oct 1) and Google ad libraries remain open, but turning them into an AI-generated-persuasion dataset is still a build-it-and-label-it project, not a deposit.

*Meta-run note:* fourth straight frontier-3 null; the AI-persuasion-text corners are comprehensively logged or structurally un-releasable (aggregates, withheld dual-use data, build-your-own ad-library labeling). Same diagnosis; flagging, not editing the rotation.
