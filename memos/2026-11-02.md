The strong hobbyist AI hoards are logged, and this is the third straight null here. The day before the midterms the AI-election-content activity is intense — but it's all **commercial trackers, academic datasets, or the logged MIT panel**, not the quiet individual collections this frontier is for.

The deepfake trackers are companies. **Resemble AI** runs a Deepfake Incident Database and a midterms watchlist ([Resemble](https://www.resemble.ai/resources/deepfake-watchlist-2026-midterms)); Cybernews analyzed 104 deepfakes from it ([Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/ai-news/political-deepfakes-2026-midterms/)); **NewsGuard** and **Presenc.ai** run their own 2026 misinformation trackers. All are commercial/editorial incident databases, PDID-adjacent (08-18), not open collector hoards. The **MIT LLM Election Observatory** ([MIT](https://news.mit.edu/2026/qa-hey-ai-who-should-i-vote-for-this-year-1030)) reruns chatbot election questions on a dashboard — the MIT election panel already logged on 09-07, not a new object. And **WMP**'s ~170 AI ads / $80M is the aggregates-only tracking rejected repeatedly.

The academic sets don't fit either. Chang et al. built a **239,526-image Instagram corpus** of 2024-election AI-generated content ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00934) adjacent) — genuinely large and on-point, but it's a research dataset with no public release link found (and 2024, not fresh); **ArPoMeme** ([LREC 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20967)) is a peer-reviewed annotated Arabic political-meme corpus, constructed and not AI-generation-specific. Neither is an individual-collector hoard, and the Instagram set isn't openly released.

No genuinely new hobbyist hoard surfaced: the political-AI corners of this frontier (jailbreaks, refusals, system prompts, political-bias/answer tracking, AI-slop cleanup, decensored weights) are comprehensively logged, and the live midterm activity consolidates into commercial incident trackers and academic corpora, not quiet individual collections.

*Meta-run note:* third straight frontier-6 null; same structural picture as the mid/late-October cycle. The one strong near-term prospect remains a watch item pending release (the Stanford/USC government-AI-chatbot-logs FOIA corpus), not a new per-frontier hobbyist hoard. Flagging, not editing the rotation.
