# Dataset Hunt — 2026-10-13

**Frontier:** 6. Collector / hobbyist archives — the Derek Willis genre: individuals quietly hoarding structured data nobody has exploited
**Verdict:** Null day — nothing cleared the bar.

Fifth pass on frontier 6 (08-14 L1B3RT4S jailbreak hoard / Civitai / Pixiv; 08-24 SpeechMap refusals / leaked system prompts / TrackingAI political bias; 09-03 WikiProject AI Cleanup; 09-23 abliterated/decensored open models). This is the richest frontier, but the strong AI hoards are now logged, and today's fresh candidates fail on **object-type collision with a logged find, or wrong-frontier / already-claimed.** I screened the frontier and promoted none — a null beats a padded find.

The best genuine Willis-genre hoard I found is a near-duplicate of one already in the ledger. **swissprismia/llm-political-bias-bench** ([GitHub](https://github.com/swissprismia/llm-political-bias-bench)) is exactly the right shape — an individual running a political-bias battery on frontier LLMs on the 1st of every month via GitHub Actions, publishing dated JSON reports and a methodology changelog — but it's an open-source version of the same object as **TrackingAI**, the longitudinal LLM political-bias tracker logged on 08-24. Its siblings are the same genre: **KorbinY/LLM-Partisan-Leaning** ([GitHub](https://github.com/KorbinY/LLM-Partisan-Leaning)) covers 43 LLMs but releases only a demo subset (provider terms restrict the raw data), and **washingtonpost/political-bias-llm-eval** is a one-off. Logging a fourth LLM-political-bias tracker (after TrackingAI, SpeechMap, and the 09-07 MIT election panel) is mode collapse, not a find.

The loudest genuinely new AI-information object isn't a hobbyist hoard and isn't unclaimed. **Grokipedia**, xAI's AI-generated encyclopedia, is a politically charged knowledge source — but it's a corporate product, not a collector's archive, and it's already been intensively studied in 2026: a PNAS paper analyzed ~18,000 matched article pairs, an arXiv preprint built a 17,790-pair corpus, and others ran LLM-judge neutrality audits ([TCD](https://www.tcd.ie/news_events/articles/2026/grokipedia-research/); [arXiv 2510.26899](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26899)) — with no open dataset released. Wrong frontier, and the opposite of unclaimed. And the **AI influence-operations** material is OpenAI's own threat reporting on China-linked ChatGPT clusters ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/prc-linked-influence-operations-ai-debates/)) — company takedown reports, not an open community database, and the influence-ops genre was logged 08-31.

*Pattern note:* sixth straight null, all on 5th–6th-visit frontiers; 10-02 (F5) and 10-05 (F8) were the last genuine finds. The rotation is picked-over on short revisit cycles — flagged repeatedly now for the Sunday meta-run; not acting unilaterally.
