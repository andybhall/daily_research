The corporate-governance legs are logged and the frontier was already null last visit. Today's two live corners fail the familiar way: **no deposited dataset, or an annual refresh of something already in the ledger.**

The one genuinely un-mined sub-area — **DAO votes** — is all proposal and experiment, no record. 2026 produced a wave of AI-agent-voting ideas: Vitalik Buterin's personal-AI "stewards" to lift DAO turnout ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/web3/2026/02/21/ethereum-s-vitalik-buterin-proposes-ai-stewards-to-help-reinvent-dao-governance)), Near's AI "digital twins," an Optimism-funded autonomous voting agent, and Valory's Governatooorr casting binary votes with users' tokens. But there is **no on-chain dataset tracking how often AI agents actually vote or move treasuries** — and, as with AI traders on Polymarket (10-06), identifying which on-chain votes are AI-cast is a contested labeling problem, not a deposited field. It's also the same object the 08-27 note already flagged (AI agents as autonomous participants in markets and governance). A build-it-from-Snapshot/Tally project with a labeling gap, not a find.

The other corner is a refresh, not a new object. The 2026 proxy season drew **~20 AI-related shareholder proposals (up from 18 in 2025, 12 in 2024), with 0–26% support (median ~10%)**, broadening past board oversight into AI misinformation, generative-AI privacy, data-center energy, military/dual-use AI, and workforce automation ([Harvard corpgov](https://corpgov.law.harvard.edu/2026/10/13/2026-proxy-season-review-submission-of-rule-14a-8-shareholder-proposals/); [Glass Lewis](https://www.glasslewis.com/article/four-observations-about-us-shareholder-proposals-in-proxy-season-2026)). That's the annual update of the AI shareholder-resolutions object logged on 08-17, and the vote data lives in commercial proxy databases (Glass Lewis, ISS, Boardroom Alpha) — not a new open deposit.

*Meta-run flag:* ninth straight null, all on 5th–6th-visit frontiers (last finds 10-02, 10-05). Same diagnosis as the past week — revisit cadence, not dead frontiers. Flagging; not acting unilaterally.
