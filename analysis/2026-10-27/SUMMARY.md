# 2026-10-27 — Bangladesh AI political-misinformation fact-check register (Mendeley h864gr3dyt)

**Object:** A curated, coded register of fact-checks of AI-generated/AI-edited political
misinformation during Bangladesh's 2025–2026 election-cycle information environment — the
non-US, non-English counterpart to the US PDID (08-18), Brazilian (09-24), and Canadian (08-28)
deepfake finds.

## Verified this run (from the Mendeley record page, fetched 2026-10-27)
- **122 item-level fact-check records**, each a unique verified claim involving AI-generated or
  AI-edited visuals/audio tied to a political actor or election process; + 13 aggregate monitoring
  sources (not counted as cases); 11 records flagged for review/exclusion/duplicates.
- **Source breakdown:** Rumor Scanner 110, Dismislab 6, FactWatch 4, BOOM 1, Ajker Patrika 1.
- **Coded fields:** source, year, English claim descriptor, synthetic-media format, dominant
  narrative frame, audit status, retrieval URL.
- **Period:** 1 Jan 2025 – 20 Feb 2026. **Country:** Bangladesh.
- **License:** CC BY 4.0. **Published:** 3 Aug 2026 (v1), DOI 10.17632/h864gr3dyt.1; compiler
  Moshiur Rahman (Comilla University); funder IAMCR.
- **Method:** manual, document-based curation of real fact-checks (purposive register; the
  compiler notes initial screening totals were not recorded, so it is not a prevalence estimate).

## Why it clears
Real-world AI-misinfo artifacts verified under fact-checking stakes (a4); curated but accreting
across the election cycle, PDID-style (b3); brand-new, single-compiler, Bengali-language — no
secondary analysis (c4, unclaimed); panel-able by source × format × frame × year × audit status,
though small N=122 (d3); bullseye Information (AI political misinfo + counter-framing) and genuinely
non-US (e5). **Score 19/25.**

## Honest caveats
- Small (122 records) and curated (purposive, not a systematic prevalence sample).
- File-level format/frame counts weren't pulled this run (would need the CSV); the chart uses the
  verified per-outlet breakdown.
