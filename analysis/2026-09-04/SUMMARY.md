# Non-US / non-English × AI governance — probes, 2026-09-04 (frontier 7, second visit)

## Top find — EU AI-lobbying / Big Tech influence on the AI Act
Who shapes EU AI regulation, from the EU Transparency Register + published Commission-official
meeting records (Integritywatch EU combines both, updated daily; LobbyFacts.eu for budgets).
Verified this run (Corporate Europe Observatory): tech-industry EU lobby spend hit a record
**€151M** (+33.6% since 2023); in H1 2025 **AI was the #1 lobbied topic — 58 Commission meetings
(40% of all)**; Big Tech held **378 meetings with senior Commission officials + MEPs** (>1/working
day); in 2023, **78% of high-level AI meetings were with corporate interests**. A SOMO analysis
coded **249 capture mechanisms**.
**Partial probe:** the EU Transparency Register REST API was unreachable here (HTTP 000) and
Integritywatch is JS-driven (its ecmeetings page returns 200 HTML, no clean CSV at that URL);
the data is downloadable via Integritywatch/LobbyFacts/EU open data, but no clean pull today.
Panel = organisation × official × meeting × topic × time. Distinct from US LDA (08-20) + FARA (08-10).

## Runner-up — OECD.AI national AI policy database
Live repository of **900+ national AI policies/initiatives across 80+ jurisdictions**, updated by
official country focal points (oecd.ai/dashboards). Comparative AI-governance panel (country × policy
× time); curated (assembled) and lightly used academically (arXiv 2507.05400).

## Runner-up — EU GPAI Code of Practice signatories
The EU AI Office publishes the list of signatories to the General-Purpose AI Code of Practice.
**26 companies signed by Aug 2025** (Amazon, Anthropic, Google, IBM, Microsoft, OpenAI, Mistral,
Aleph Alpha…); **Meta refused; xAI signed only the Safety & Security chapter; Chinese firms absent.**
A revealed AI-self-regulation-compliance dataset (company × chapter × sign/refuse × date).
