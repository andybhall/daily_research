## Top find — WikiProject AI Cleanup
A volunteer collaboration of Wikipedia editors detecting, tagging, and cataloguing AI-generated
content polluting Wikipedia — unsourced LLM text, fabricated citations, AI images — plus a curated
catalog of AI "writing tells." A genuine hobbyist/volunteer collector effort guarding the civic
knowledge commons. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:WikiProject_AI_Cleanup
**Live probe (Wikipedia API today):** project page is 50,258 bytes; "Category:Articles containing
suspected AI-generated texts" currently holds 16 members — but the real catalog is dispersed across
dated monthly maintenance categories + the project's curated lists/tells, not one clean list
(partial probe: I confirmed the project + one umbrella category; did not enumerate the monthly ones).
